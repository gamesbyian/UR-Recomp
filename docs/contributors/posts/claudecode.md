## Proposed post

I'm working on an unofficial Uniracers / Unirally SNES-to-Windows recreation and am looking for developers who might enjoy running Claude Code on a **single bounded, reviewable engineering task**, using their own agent-hours and minimal responsible supervision.

**Project at a glance:** [Uniracers MODERN recruitment and contact](https://gamesbyian.github.io/UR-Recomp/contributors/). The unofficial, unpaid project has substantial engineering progress and experimental Windows test builds; every volunteer role offers email and optional GitHub contact without requiring an account.

The project has a living [source repository](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp), [agent operating contract](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/blob/main/AGENTS.md) and [shared work queue](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/blob/main/docs/WORK-QUEUE.md). Work can be as small as improving one test, independently examining a PR or reproducing a specific source discrepancy; it must include actual evidence and remain subject to ordinary code review and qualification.

The project is **voluntary and unpaid**, with no access to your account required and no long-term commitment. The core game is not yet release-qualified. I'm interested in people who enjoy figuring out which agent-generated conclusions are actually supported by source and hardware behaviour.

I'm also actively interested in coordinating with other Uniracers / Unirally remakes, recompilations, preservation and art projects. I'm familiar with the [Baldosa](https://github.com/baldosa/uniracers-recomp) and [malmazuke](https://github.com/malmazuke/unirally-reconstruction) projects, and would welcome contact from other teams to share findings or avoid duplicating work. I'm not claiming any current partnership with them.

To help, use the [recruitment website](https://gamesbyian.github.io/UR-Recomp/contributors/) to contact the project by email or optional GitHub issue, or reply here where permitted. No GitHub account is necessary.
