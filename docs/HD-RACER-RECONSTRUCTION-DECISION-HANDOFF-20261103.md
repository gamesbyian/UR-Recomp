## Next experiment: registration before geometry

**Decision to resolve:** Can a *shared* camera and temporally coherent rig pose sequence, with documented red display reflection and player palette conventions, explain successive authenticated source images materially better than the existing five unordered GLB silhouettes, without per-frame camera/shape cheating?

1. **Reuse immutable source:** take #3088's executed no-skip Mesen 20-tick archive and SHA-checked 40 source/screen masks; inspect #3094/#3096/#3097 state. Use 15 source-only exemplars for human inspection **only** if their exact SHA receipts qualify; do not equate tile-disjoint content with independent camera/pose classes. Retain all 40 as the scoring census. No new emulator compile, replay, original ZIP or reference scrape unless a proposed decision cannot be answered with current bytes.
2. **Pre-register comparison arms on fit-only evidence:** A, baseline five GLB silhouettes under existing frozen provisional camera; B, same geometry, a single shared camera plus a **globally fixed** blue/red model reflection convention; C, same geometry/camera and constrained continuous rig phases/lean keyed to source *observed chronological neighbors* (not presumed 1994 skeleton joints). Do not introduce new geometry variables until camera/reflection and temporal registration ablations have been evaluated. Separately record whether an original pair is chronologically adjacent and truly changes; avoid fabricated frame interpolation.
3. **Explicit degrees-of-freedom budget:** one camera hypothesis for a declared run/viewport family, fixed player reflection/palette convention, shared geometry and pivots, only mechanically plausible per-time rig parameters with a smoothness/complexity prior. No best-of-pose for each source without recording selection entropy, no per-image free scale/translation/flip or manual pose labels inferred from the highest IoU. Use the original lower-visible alpha as an image registration *anchor*, never assert it is ground contact. Preserve 7:6 aspect and completed PPU orientation.
4. **Scoring:** report per-image IoU, symmetric boundary distance (and distance units), source landmark/painted-axis residual with uncertainty, false-colour/palette area distribution, source-visible part/occlusion discrepancies where actually identifiable, temporal jump/continuity penalty, parameter count, runtime and failure cases. Aggregate **one vote per independent source-content family** and separately disclose raw 40-image means; do not let repeated or nearly identical frames dominate. Report source-role strata and uncertainty/ambiguous best fits. Include per-player breakdown, especially the blue residual left after red reflection.
5. **Leakage gate:** freeze historical 16/19/strict-six roles. The 38 new source planes are **exploration/diagnostics only** unless a future prospective split is committed *before* fitting and excludes exact/near-duplicate and temporal neighbors across roles. Earlier 40-image inspection precludes claiming these same images as previously unseen prospective blind tests. A new independently executed source sequence/view is justified only after the model, hypotheses and heldout membership are committed and a concrete generalization decision depends on it.
6. **Compare cheap first:** offline source SHA and shape contracts, synthetic projection/reflection/pose identifiability tests, then cached renders of the five real GLBs, then one bounded fit study. Cache by model SHA + camera SHA + pose parameter vector + projection settings. Do not rerun actual Mesen or export every geometry variation if pre-existing real GLB projections and exact inputs answer the question. Use HP for many CPU-bound offline candidates only after profiling a single genuine experiment; CI should run fast contracts by default and a single opt-in heavy exact-head producer for accepted experimental changes.
7. **Independent review and stop rule:** predeclare a quantitative improvement threshold using synthetic noise/reliability controls before seeing the withheld values. Require improvement across independent source families and both players, not just a pooled mean, plus no material loss in temporal continuity, landmarks and material/pivot validity. If the frozen-camera/temporal hypotheses are indistinguishable or worse, **record the negative result and stop**. Resolve source-pose ambiguity with a genuinely discriminating reference or re-examine camera/observation assumptions rather than adding unconstrained geometry. No automatic GLB default change on unpaired silhouette gain.
