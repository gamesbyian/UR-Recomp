## Proposed post

I'm developing an unofficial Windows recreation of the 1994 Uniracers / Unirally SNES game and would appreciate volunteer Windows testers who can identify crashes, controller issues and differences from the original.

Areas of interest: single-player and two-player controls, game feel, camera readability, sound, menu navigation, and reliably reproducible defects. The [formal playtest guidance](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/blob/main/docs/EXPERT-PLAYTEST-PROTOCOL.md) explains how to submit one finding at a time without uploading a ROM or personal log data.

This is **unpaid, voluntary** testing, with no compensation expected and no obligation to continue. This is a pre-release experimental recreation, not a finished commercial game.

I'm also actively interested in coordinating with any other Uniracers / Unirally projects. I already know the [Baldosa](https://github.com/baldosa/uniracers-recomp) and [malmazuke](https://github.com/malmazuke/unirally-reconstruction) efforts and would welcome shared findings, source comparisons and contact with additional teams. No partnership with those projects is implied.
