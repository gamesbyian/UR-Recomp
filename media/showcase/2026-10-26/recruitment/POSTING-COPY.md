## Flagship description

Uniracers / Unirally was an extraordinary 1994 SNES racer. UR-Recomp is an independent effort to reconstruct the original game faithfully for modern Windows PCs, preserving its behavior while rebuilding the presentation, menus and surrounding systems.

This film includes genuine native widescreen two-player footage from three separately captured courses: **MegaJump, Jumps and Looper**. It also shows the original-to-expanded-world reveal, selected engineering comparisons, evidence of an Original-mode UHD framebuffer, and a candid look at the experimental 4× racer artwork. The procedural 4× characters still need to be replaced by faithful, artist-approved reconstruction.

**Three ways to help:**

1. **Windows playtesters.** Help independently test controllers, split-screen racing, video modes, sound, modern menus, records and safe restart/recovery on real PCs.
2. **2D/3D artists.** Help model, rig, render and animate the original rounded CGI unicycle characters at modern density, and contribute interface, track and compositing artwork.
3. **Coding-agent contributors.** Put AI development hours toward a ranked set of meaningful engineering tasks. The repository documents tasks, validation, review and handoffs in AGENTS.md and docs/HOURLY-AGENT-LINES.md.

**Code and contributor entry point:** https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp  
**Contact:** ianmakesjokes@gmail.com

This is an unofficial research/remaster project. The Windows product remains under development and independent release validation is incomplete. The trailer's 3840×2160 proof concerns bounded **Original-mode output**, not fully approved HD artwork or filmed finished 4K gameplay. The three racing excerpts are from separate genuine sessions; no fully completed Looper revolution is claimed.

No ROM license, game copyright or distribution rights are claimed by the project.
