# Box-art wheel contour studies

Two user-supplied, interpretive line/outline studies over close crops of Uniracers / Unirally box artwork. These are visual artist references for reconstructing silhouette, tire profile, fork geometry, wheel rim, spokes, hub, pedal and adjacent mechanical-looking elements.

**Source custody:** files were moved from the repository root, without modifying their bytes, and assigned descriptive names based on the source filenames. The overlays and glints are image interpretation and drawing effects, **not confirmed original mesh seams, topology, or proof of 1994 3D construction**. Use alongside the [title-screen racer reference set](../../2026-11-09/title-screen-racer/README.md) and the [3D reconstruction lab](https://gamesbyian.github.io/UR-Recomp/contributors/3d/).
