# Original title-screen racer / contour reference set

**Purpose:** Art-facing source-image reference for refining the experimental 2026 articulated unicycle racer. These four supplied PNG files are moved here **without any pixel edits or re-encoding** from the repository root. They depict a 3× upscaled rendering of the racer's appearance in the game's title-screen artwork plus three interpretive contour/outline studies of the full image and its crops.

This is a visual target for silhouette, part boundaries, saddle curvature, seatpost/collar, fork/hub, pedals, wheel and colored surfaces. It is **not a recovered original 1994 3D model**, and the drawn contour separations are **artistic hypotheses**, not measured topology, engineering proof or evidence that particular parts were originally independent meshes. Do not infer hidden 3D geometry from a painted line. No new gameplay render is implied.

## Art direction use

Compare the 2026 [interactive 3D viewer](https://gamesbyian.github.io/UR-Recomp/model-review/), the [experiment and downloadable model-source overview](https://gamesbyian.github.io/UR-Recomp/contributors/3d/), and these images side by side. First assess the main racer outline, then identify saddle, seatpost and collar relationships, fork/hub shapes, wheel/tire and pedal geometry. A close-up may overemphasize strokes or ambiguous junctions; always refer back to the full title-screen composition.

The project is unofficial fan work. The underlying game imagery is third-party copyrighted material and remains reference-only. Do not treat the files as free stock assets or as evidence of permission for unrelated redistribution. Avoid including the full images in a game release without a rights review.
