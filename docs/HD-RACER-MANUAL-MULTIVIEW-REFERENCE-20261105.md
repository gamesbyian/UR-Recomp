## Why this already-retained publication matters

The owner's [character likeness brief](HD-RACER-CHARACTER-RECONSTRUCTION.md) calls for authentic large-form design rather than an arbitrary model fitted only to 64px ROM silhouettes. The official 1994 Nintendo/DMA instruction booklet contains **several different rendered character viewpoints**, including a relatively large near-wheel oblique illustration, a fork-facing view and stunts/side poses. These are potentially much stronger constraints on the 2026 candidate's fork crown, rim, hub, saddle underside and crank assembly than another tiny gameplay silhouette adjustment.

This is **not a newly acquired asset**: the repo already retains `reference/imported/manuals/Uniracers-USA-manual.pdf`. An independent public read used [the matching US manual scan](https://www.videogamemanual.com/snes/Uniracers%20%28USA%29.pdf), a printed original Nintendo 1994 manual (front matter ©1994 Nintendo/DMA). No redistributable scan, image cutout, redraw or unverified new 3D model is committed with this note. Before numeric measurement compare **exact source PDF bytes/page render** in repo against the public scan; the repo PDF's complete SHA and any crop SHA have **not** been measured here, and should not be invented. The source is official **printed CGI-style artwork**, but provenance does not establish that it used exactly the original gameplay animation mesh, camera or light rig.

## Physical reconstruction questions sharpened by these views

1. **Saddle-to-crown clamp and head thickness.** In the current 2026 GLB/SCAD, saddle, fork and their attachment pivots are structurally separate but physically schematic. Printed p. 14 and 16 offer cross-direction constraints on the rounded black saddle underside, projecting stem, metal clamp and bulky fork crown. An apparently shorter/wider head in one view may be foreshortening, not a width scale.
2. **Wheel–hub–fork intersection.** The pronounced hub, multiple relatively broad metallic spokes, fork branches and crank/pedal in p. 15 help address ambiguous 2D colour ownership. Earlier [#3055](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/3055) found source high-chroma paint extensively occupying the 2D wheel disc; later [#3159](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/3159) checked that effect on 48 actual source Mesen PPU images. These coloured screen pixels do not prove a coloured wheel or fork; need source-illustration-guided 3D material/part alternatives under the **same** authored model. [#3160](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/3160) tests pure material allocation without any new mesh.
3. **Wheel depth and rim/tyre cross-section.** The large p. 15 oblique view shows an actual *thick tire volume* rather than a planar silhouette. Its near/far rim and hub are differently visible. The old 64×64 source image gives less depth information. The current 2026 tire torus geometry and thin wire-like spoke shapes are provisional; original printed perspective must be tagged before proposing changed 3D thickness or spokes.
4. **Consistent anatomical identity across views.** Page 14's fork-facing silhouette and p. 15's oblique underside can reject a candidate that fits one sideways frame by hiding extra volumes in depth. Both are needed before a new mesh candidate moves from draft research to artistic review.

## Evidence boundaries

Official printed illustrations can establish visibly distinct printed **2D outlines and shading**. They cannot prove missing 1994 3D source files, original face ordering, joint rig, objective wheel physics, arbitrary interpolated poses, real-time animation or source renderer/camera. These images are not a prospective blind 3D holdout until every source/view/crop and fit/test decision is frozen. Character likeness still requires explicit **owner visual acceptance** separate from the working original renderer, QA score and Windows release gate.
