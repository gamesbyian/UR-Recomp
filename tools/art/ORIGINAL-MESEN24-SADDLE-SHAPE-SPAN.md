# Original 1994 racer silhouette versus 2026 saddle mesh span

**Scientific purpose:** Resolve whether the current 2026 five-parent unicycle blockout's large, character-defining saddle contour is geometrically too long or short after prior wheel, stem-position and world-attitude motion controls. The original programmer's period 3D mesh is missing; 64×64 source sprites constrain projected pixels, not a unique original 3D shape.

**One original geometry, reversible in-memory shape experiment.** The real source-owned 2026 GLB remains the sole rig, with its actual two original saddle material meshes, original parent pivot and rest pose. Scale only saddle *local longitudinal X vertices* by two frozen **0.90 and 1.10** dimensionless factors. Original saddle Y/Z, its mount and all other four parent meshes/transform matrices, paints, wheel ground contact, source camera and the original pre-rendered ROM remain untouched. These are exact before-test controls, chosen symmetrically around 1.0 rather than optimized per racer or source tick. Both factors are crossed with the previously validated fixed source phase×15° and fixed chronological +24° wheel drives: four additional original-source comparison arms.

**Rollback:** For each real GLB projection, temporarily modify the exact two GLB saddle geometry vertex arrays, retain original 2026 triangle faces and all vertex RGBA/material node identities, verify all five parent world matrices unchanged, project, and restore original vertex arrays even on an exception. No stored GLB or SCAD is replaced, no second model, 3D camera or source asset is generated. A dependency-free synthetic test rejects arbitrary/fitted factors, nonfinite coordinates, invalid vertices and keeps X-only behaviour exact.

**Measurement:** Evaluate all 48 original Mesen source frames (two players × 24 genuine contiguous ticks), genuine 23 chronological source change events per player, source image-space upper/middle/lower TP/FP/FN, one-vote-per-distinct-original-silhouette static IoU, and the two previously identified upper change events with exclusive and ambiguous model parent visible-motion attribution. Preserve the exact original 16 previous 2D/GLB control results, original source SHA/frozen 7:6 camera/one per-player first-tick registration; require actual GLB projected five-parent OR silhouette equals the exact predeclared whole exported model for each model variation. If blue benefits but red degrades, or upper motion worsens while static silhouette improves, record **mixed or negative**, not an optimized historical width. No retrospective frame can be relabelled a blind holdout.

**Acceptance constraints:** This research can recommend one authored 2026 shape modification for the user's visual judgement but cannot recover a unique 1994 original surface from the sprites, prove 1994 PBR material identity, or override the explicitly rejected blockout likeness. Keep draft #2832 unapproved, ART/Windows release gates unchanged, and review through the sole Pages model viewer once evidence justifies a display control. Qualification uses existing full tooling/hygiene and actual OpenSCAD GLB/Chromium Windows Visual Atlas job, without adding CI workflow infrastructure.
