The Hidden Palace-local download is currently absent, but the page exposes the Forest of Illusion external mirror URL above. The current execution environment was unable to transfer that RAR, so the bytes are not yet in this repository. The exact archive URL and filename are preserved here so a future acquisition attempt does not need to rediscover them.

MAME 0.247 release notes independently list `Unirally (Euro, prototype)` as a newly working SNES software-list addition credited to Zoda-Y13 and Forest of Illusion. This provides another route to obtain canonical hashes/ROM naming even if the original archive disappears.

Once acquired, keep the untouched RAR plus extracted ROM/chip files, record all hashes, and compare against PAL retail and the US baseline.
