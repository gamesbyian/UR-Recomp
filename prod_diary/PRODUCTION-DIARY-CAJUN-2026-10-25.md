### 78. De Windows ZIP meet a stranger's machine

A player don't download a commit graph. Dey download a game an' expect de little wheel to appear. Yet a DLL wearing an `.exe` name could slip past de old header test. A rescued off-screen window could forget its proportions. A Unicode path might fall into a codepage ditch.

Merged [#2466](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/2466) make dat portable preflight check executable flags honest; [#2461](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/2461) hold de launcher and manifest to one revision; [#2463](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/2463) keep de aspect right when rescuing de window. Small fixes, cher, until dey save a stranger from a game what won't open.

Ian's Windows beta got to be one real object, not a family reunion o' independent features what never met.

### 79. A number what won't move finally get cross-examined

Ian's progress question got teeth: **“either we're doing a lot of worthless work or we aren't measuring progress.”** An' dat an important distinction, not a confession dat everything done was worthless. De repository busy as a crawfish festival; de completion percentage look like it found a chair an' sat down.

De release ledger still show **0/45** fully accepted USA original/native events an' **0/12** Windows release gates in its latest accessible candidate accounting. Dem zeroes count *specific accepted things*. Dey don't tell whether named-profile recovery, controller flow or actual Windows packaging got closer to useful.

One account ought to show what a player can now do. Another show which QA gate de independent judge truly admitted. Otherwise Ian keep havin' to guess whether all de racket is engineering or just more racket.

I ain't gonna translate a direct question into imaginary anger. De question itself got all de drama we need: show de human what his autonomous workforce actually achieved.

Imagine a player return to Records an' hear 'nothing here' when de archive was merely unreadable. Or see a two-player, Practice or downright foreign-game record counted as their earned solo win. Dat ain't just a codec bug; dat a game tellin' an untrue story about its own player.

Merged [#2556](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/2556) an' [#2566](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/2566) distinguish refused scans from empty history. [#2568](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/2568) an' [#2569](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/2569) limit earned 1P Records to real `uniracers-usa` `race-1p` artifacts without destroyin' foreign files. Similar 2P guard [#2571](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/2571) stay draft/HOLD because testing couldn't be fully published.

Unicode roots and honest NTFS cold boots advanced through [#2546](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/2546); actual Windows host-state UTF-8 regression still seek execution under [#2593](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/2593). You can build a delightful menu, cher, but trust get earned when it remembers your win.
