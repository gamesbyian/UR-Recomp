# ART-4K: real original-ROM full-silhouette lean check

**Scope:** one existing source-authenticated, five-part / sixteen-material 2026 OpenSCAD GLB, one fixed and explicitly *provisional* camera, five opt-in source-world Y lean rotations and canonical original USA-ROM sprite alpha masks. No new rig, viewer, model, original animation-frame mapping or art approval.

## Why this study matters

The earlier 20-angle grid measured the direction of coloured fork pixels, not the entire character. Its angular coverage improved dramatically with a few lean previews, but a thin visible axis can match while wheel, fork, saddle and pedal silhouettes are badly wrong. This follow-up tests the complete geometry against **all 35 SHA-distinct original 64x64 alpha masks**. It consumes authenticated original-ROM raster corpus and the historical exact upright GLB, both already restored by the bounded original-family research workflow.

It tests the **five prior declared viewer angles**, unchanged 0°, −35°, +10°, +15° and +30°, without adjusting angles based on the resulting whole-silhouette scores. The pivot is the experimental source-world origin, not independently recovered game physics. Every rotation acts on all 28,860 source GLB triangles, after baking its five editable parts' world transforms. The whole model is moved uniformly only enough to preserve the original experimental **tire lower source-Z**. Other parts may still intersect an imagined ground plane; the report records the whole model's source-Z minimum and refuses to infer original collision mechanics.

## Registration and scoring

Every candidate is rasterized at 4× resolution through the identical SHA-pinned provisional 7:6 source camera with **no per-sprite camera optimization**. It is reduced from the rasterized 256×256 source pixel centers to original 64×64, and only the preexisting independently authenticated *visible original-alpha* lower contact midpoint translation is applied, at exact half-pixel resolution, to the candidate for each source shape. That contact was measured from the original ROM sprite, **not** inferred to be the true physical wheel contact.

One score per unique source binary alpha hash reports whole-silhouette intersection-over-union, exclusive-or pixels, 7:6 physical-pixel boundary chamfer, and remaining lowest-alpha contact difference. Historical frame holds do not multiply one binary shape into many fit votes; source-player IDs and full representation identities are retained separately.

The existing original source-window connected-component split is preserved for leakage diagnostics. **It is not an unseen validation result**: the same 35 original masks were inspected when selecting the four lean angles by the earlier painted-axis experiment. Resulting scores compare *source-observed full 2D geometric coverage of fixed 2026 proposals*, not recovered 1994 3D motion or silhouette likeness.

Its added step runs the real complete-mesh projections and original-PNG metrics, refuses missing or mismatched bytes and uploads the resulting JSON alongside the current original motion, chroma-axis, near-duplicate and wheel-ablation reports. No fresh scheduled workflow or heavy native game run is introduced.

ROM-free contract tests verify allowed angles, a reversible root geometry transformation, one-vote-per-source-shape aggregation, duplicate/invalid source score rejection, and preservation of the original experimental tire lower height. A separate run must produce real GLB/source results before comparing or making any recommendation.

**Next decision:** if improved painted-axis angles do not improve complete-silhouette IoU and residual contact errors, stop chasing the scalar lean score and refit the model's actual source-owned wheel, fork and saddle proportions with a genuinely withheld original semantic pose family. Keep source original-camera, original animation correspondence, geometry recovery and owner character-likeness approval false until explicitly demonstrated.

## Observed real-source result, GitHub Actions #41357381721

The **actual** original USA-ROM producer completed on the first PR head. The seven-file numeric ZIP artifact #13103667975 has SHA-256 `4890e9d9004b553e6649d1f8484fc05082653bfd7418eb8e8d22df575e86f165`, passes CRC verification and contains `full-lean-silhouette.json` (SHA-256 `efec65d65db40ac866d217714acfce657036f7ccd092849255efefb67e32dfee`). The report reverified all 35 unique original source binary masks, SHA-pinned actual five-part GLB and unchanged frozen camera, and rasterized the complete 28,860-triangle source candidate five times.

The original unchanged model is the highest unpaired-IoU fixed candidate for **31 of the 35** original silhouettes; +10° leads only four. The lean-only trials are worse overall across three different full-shape metrics, even though the separate painted-fork-axis study preferred some of these lean directions.

The evidence also isolates a **source-visible contact-placement issue**: original contact-registered candidate alpha bounds are consistently one logical pixel vertically above the source (half-pixel unit residual y2=−2). For −35°, candidate lowest-alpha horizontal residual is x2=−9 across every source shape, +10° gives +3, +15° gives +4 and +30° gives +8. These are per-*study pose* residuals common to all registered shapes, not independent proof of 1994 game wheel physics, and no camera tweak or per-pose source alignment was performed in the test. Fitting source-owned art proportions and modeling actual contact should precede adopting these root rotations.

**Decision:** retain the reversible viewer control for qualitative reference, **do not promote the angle-only numeric optimum into the canonical rig**. Use the original source mask IoU, boundary and contact witnesses when evaluating further geometry/kinematic changes. All original 1994 camera/3D pose identity, artist likeness and shipping-art gates remain unapproved.
