## Original proposed title

[Unpaid / Hobby] Looking for a human-made 3D racing unicycle model for a Uniracers SNES recreation

## Original proposed post (reference copy, not verified against the published Reddit body)

I'm working on **Uniracers MODERN**, an unofficial, completely non-commercial recreation of the 1994 SNES game Uniracers / Unirally for Windows.

I'm looking for help sourcing or creating a **human-made 3D model of the game's racing unicycle character**.

The character is effectively a living, expressive unicycle, with distinctive wheel, fork, axle, saddle and pedal geometry. It needs to be recognizable from multiple angles and eventually support pedalling, leaning, jumping, flipping and other racing animations.

The project has reverse-engineering and engineering work underway, plus original artwork references, enlarged sprites, interpretive contour studies and an experimental five-part 3D rig. The provisional rig is optional reference material, not an approved original 1994 model.

Help could involve a properly licensed existing model, a volunteer original sculpt, pointers to appropriate artists or assets, or technical advice on the reconstruction.

**Visual references and model downloads:** https://gamesbyian.github.io/UR-Recomp/contributors/3d/

**Recruitment/contact site:** https://gamesbyian.github.io/UR-Recomp/contributors/

This is an **unpaid**, entirely voluntary fan project, independent of original rights holders. The project is also keen to coordinate with other independent Uniracers / Unirally efforts, including Baldosa and malmazuke, without implying any affiliation.

People can reach the coordinator through the site's email link, without needing a GitHub account.
