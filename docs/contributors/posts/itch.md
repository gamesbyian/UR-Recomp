## Proposed post

Hi! I'm coordinating an unofficial Uniracers / Unirally SNES-to-Windows recreation and looking for volunteer visual artists who enjoy either stylized 3D character construction or unusual lettering.

The work is already grounded in source and existing experiments: a five-part 3D unicycle model and detailed [artist brief](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/blob/main/docs/HD-RACER-CHARACTER-RECONSTRUCTION.md), plus traced [North American](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/blob/main/assets/branding/uniracers-box-wordmark-traced.svg) and [European](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/blob/main/assets/branding/unirally-box-wordmark-traced.svg) English title designs. [Japanese](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/blob/main/assets/branding/uniracers-japanese-katakana-wordmark.svg), [Russian](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/blob/main/assets/branding/unirally-russian-cyrillic-wordmark.svg) and [French](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/blob/main/assets/branding/unirally-french-wordmark.svg) draft SVGs could benefit from critique and refinement.

For 3D, the task is a high-fidelity articulated, living unicycle character rather than a generic wheel. For lettering, it's close attention to the original English shapes and personality while respecting how the other alphabets actually work.

This is **unpaid, voluntary and noncommercial fan collaboration** with acknowledgement and portfolio permission where rights allow. One focused contribution is perfectly welcome; nobody needs to join a permanent team. Reply with the part you'd like to help with and a public example if you have one.

I'm also actively interested in coordinating with other Uniracers / Unirally remakes, recompilations, preservation and art projects. I'm familiar with the [Baldosa](https://github.com/baldosa/uniracers-recomp) and [malmazuke](https://github.com/malmazuke/unirally-reconstruction) projects, and would welcome contact from other teams to share findings or avoid duplicating work. I'm not claiming any current partnership with them.

[Project overview](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp). This invitation will be posted personally by a human, not by an AI agent.
