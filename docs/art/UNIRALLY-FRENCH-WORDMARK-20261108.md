# French Unirally film-title wordmark

**Text:** L'Inéluctable Esseulement de la Roue Unique  
**Status:** Original humorous alternative branding proposed by the owner, **not a verified historical French title**.

The SVG `assets/branding/unirally-french-wordmark.svg` is a self-contained editable vector treatment of the complete French phrase. All accents and the apostrophe are present. The text is arranged in three lines for a deliberately theatrical title-card composition:

1. `L'Inéluctable`
2. `Esseulement`
3. `de la Roue Unique`

Its shapes are converted into SVG glyph paths from a bold decorative display face rather than left as live text. That ensures no font installation is needed and the title remains printable, portable and crisp at any resolution. The glyph shapes can be separately edited.

Brand palette: yellow `#ffea00`, black `#090409`, diagonal offset (+2,+2) with black stroke width 8, and centered black keyline width 2.4, matching the English wordmark's black/yellow treatment. The layout and decorative French typographic personality deliberately differ from the compact English title to accommodate its much longer phrase.

The English, Japanese and Russian regional artwork remains unchanged. This French wordmark is independent, and the title's mock-literary provenance is intentionally distinguished from historically attested localization evidence.

No original packaging vector master, owner acceptance of the exact layout, or release gate is claimed.
