# Genuine original 1994 printed fork, collar and saddle-post image features

The one 2026 five-parent/16-material GLB is still **unapproved**. This investigation adds a second independent image-plane evidence class to the already source-PDF-verified saddle visual estimates (#3176). It does **not** assert that the manual's original printed rendering comes from the game's now-missing source 1994 production 3D mesh, that a cyan painted region belongs to a particular 3D subassembly, or that the production artist's camera has been calibrated.

The exact source is the already-retained Nintendo/DMA USA manual, Git blob SHA1 `a52da4f94a75e3e76fd61a759cb8e0c3a4fbc5d3`, full PDF SHA256 `50d5d02a3f8f04b9a38a1dac7ff05fd96f5583fbdf1d0afc201bbaea454e2235`. Source view p14 fork-front PNG SHA256 `cfbbc7b3e3cd1e3786897eba580bffec2ee9ceec268bcf4cdb4b5b6e30f4642b`, p15 giant low-oblique underside SHA256 `4c9242a0126891a2d6a6e8f574eec4179c4cdbcd30cb34a1cdff8589e9290d22`. Original printed scan pixel aspect is **square**, no 7:6 SNES pixel correction.

From independent visual inspection of the exact 358×369 p14 and 900×1151 p15 original cropped PNGs, record **conservative approximate image-plane extents** in `analysis/data/racer-original-manual-fork-clamp-image-traces-20261105.json`. The old four-point saddle estimates are reused byte-identically, not silently shifted. In p14, a cyan bridge/collar **visually labelled crown-like** spans approx X129–195 (66 px), the two legs **visually labelled fork-like** span X132–191 (59 px), and the turquoise seat-post collar **visually labelled clamp-like** spans Y121–146 (25 px). Per-point uncertainty is **±9 image pixels** due to scan blur, printed track overlap and component boundary ambiguity. The large p15 oblique figure exposes a turquoise structure **visually labelled crown-like** spanning X542–685 (143 px), and a dark metallic seat brace **visually labelled clamp-like** spanning Y208–288 (80 px), at deliberately generous **±22 image pixels** per landmark. These printed region labels are **hypotheses about visually separable surfaces**, not verified original physical part names, topological joints or homogeneous 3D materials.

The two source print views have independent poses, camera perspectives, figure scale, occlusions and composition. Even within p14, the visual fork span / cyan bridge span is only a **projected 2D ratio ~0.894**, not an original physical fork-spacing ratio, because there is no calibrated perspective. p15's printed clamp vertical span / cyan band horizontal span is only an **image-plane cross-feature ratio ~0.559**, even more camera-dependent. Those ratios are not robust mesh calibration constraints. Entire wheel and hub silhouette groups are occluded or blended: their complete bounds remain **unmeasurable**, and every wheel-diameter-normalized ratio remains null. No actual 1994 model has been extracted and no autonomous colour-to-material attribution is approved.
