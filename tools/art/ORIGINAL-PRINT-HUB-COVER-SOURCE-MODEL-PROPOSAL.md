# Experimental physical 2026 dark hub-cover shell, inspired by original 1994 print

This is the next **genuine three-dimensional character mesh experiment** in the **one** existing unapproved five-parent/16-material 2026 GLB research stack #3124. It is **not** a recovered original 1994 model, production camera, material map or accepted character likeness.

## Source evidence

The Nintendo/DMA original USA manual p15 giant underside render includes a **dark, angular, large central housing face** between visible metallic wheel supports, turquoise fork structure and right-side crank/pedal. The same exact original PDF and p15 PNG have been pixel-authenticated under #3166/#3170/#3174; the more recent sparse-part observations #3180 verify nearby fork/collar 2D features. The new #3181 source-polygon intake proposes one traced **exposed portion** of that dark housing, about **35,973.5 original PDF scan px²**, with explicit ±24px source-pixel boundary uncertainty and unresolved far-side hub/part ownership. This 2D visible area is **not** a 3D physical cross-section, width or depth. The original printed model may not match the 1994 game's lost production source 3D artwork.

## Exact original 2026 model and one research geometry candidate

The experimental source input is the **same real five-parent/16-material 2026 upright GLB**, SHA256 `634aa0280ec1a8a322c34d1598680e854980f317965b3a848a2cf6b240c50c08`, already retained from the successful Windows OpenSCAD/GLB/Chromium Pages model export. This particular GLB's original `rim_and_hub__material_4` is the dark existing cylindrical axle sleeve, **100 vertices, 196 triangles**, RGB 82/94/110, closed mesh with 2026-model-only volume ~**0.009733130** cubic units.

Propose **one fixed physical source-owned near-side housing cover**, shaped as a bevelled eight-corner plate with X span **−0.30…+0.22**, Z span **−0.37…+0.27**, Y near side **+0.18…+0.38** in *2026 local axle model coordinates*. This is exactly **16 new vertices and 28 new triangles** with bevel 0.04, one proposed face volume **0.06592** 2026 cubic units. The candidate attaches the cover geometry to the **existing original dark hub material mesh**. No new material nodes, no change to the original axle cylinder triangles/vertices/colours, no change to tire/rim/spokes, fork/crown, crank, pedals or saddle, and no new parent joint or canonical model. The additive source material becomes **116 vertices/224 triangles** and composite source-model material signed volume **0.075653130**, an increase of **677.274427% for that tiny dark hub material only** (never interpret as whole wheel/character volume). The original source arrays restore exactly even on interruption.

This first prototype intentionally leaves **two overlapping closed solids inside the one source material mesh**, without claiming a boolean-fused watertight single-component physical manufactured part. Such a CSG union, fastener, real pivot and physically faithful axle housing must be researched separately. Source 2026 model signed volume is not source 1994 fidelity.
