# Display and presentation FPS policy conversation transcript

Captured: 2026-10-03

Source: UR-Recomp display/output policy conversation.

The project formalized host-owned output resolution, display mode, VSync, presentation refresh/FPS and internal render scale as distinct modern-product settings. A central constraint was that presentation cadence must never change authoritative guest simulation cadence, timers, physics, AI, RNG, animation-state selection or records.

The resulting policy became the basis for the later incremental Options integrations.
