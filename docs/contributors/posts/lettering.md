## Proposed post

I'm experimenting with regional versions of the distinctive rounded, playful Uniracers / Unirally title lettering from the 1994 SNES game. I'd appreciate detailed feedback from anyone experienced in lettering or specific scripts, and, if community rules permit, a volunteer for one focused revision.

[Rectified separated English letter shapes](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/blob/main/design/uniracers-wordmark-separated-rectified.svg) (use their shapes, not the oversized U/S display scaling), [English Uniracers trace](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/blob/main/assets/branding/uniracers-box-wordmark-traced.svg) and [English Unirally trace](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/blob/main/assets/branding/unirally-box-wordmark-traced.svg) are the shape references. Existing **experimental** translations/transliterations include [Japanese katakana](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/blob/main/assets/branding/uniracers-japanese-katakana-wordmark.svg), [Russian Cyrillic](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/blob/main/assets/branding/unirally-russian-cyrillic-wordmark.svg) and an intentionally overlong [French treatment](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/blob/main/assets/branding/unirally-french-wordmark.svg).

New, supplied **styling concept images**: [concept 01](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/blob/main/assets/branding/concepts/regional-wordmark-concept-01.png), [concept 02](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/blob/main/assets/branding/concepts/regional-wordmark-concept-02.png), [concept 03](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/blob/main/assets/branding/concepts/regional-wordmark-concept-03.png). The three visual subjects are French “L'inéluctable esseulement de la roue unique”, Japanese “ユニレーサーズ” and Russian “Юниралли”; the original repository PNGs have anonymous filenames, so exact file-to-language identification must be confirmed before captions are attached individually. These are illustration references, not accepted canonical SVG lettering.

The challenge is achieving consistent rhythm, curves, contour weighting and personality without mistreating the anatomy of non-Latin scripts. Linguistic suitability should be checked separately from visual likeness. Any assistance is **unpaid and optional**, with acknowledgement where permitted. A useful critique or one glyph improvement would be plenty.

I'm interested in coordinating with any other Uniracers / Unirally remake, reverse-engineering, preservation or art project. I already know the [Baldosa](https://github.com/baldosa/uniracers-recomp) and [malmazuke](https://github.com/malmazuke/unirally-reconstruction) efforts. I would welcome shared research and additional contacts, without implying any current affiliation.
