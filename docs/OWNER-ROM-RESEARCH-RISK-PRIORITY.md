# Owner priority: research accessibility and risk acceptance

**Owner direction, 2026-10-26.** The owner understands and accepts the risks associated with ROMs and ROM-derived research material being discoverable via this public repository. The project's objective is **fast, unimpeded research and development** toward a faithful playable Uniracers remaster, not secrecy, access control for its own sake, or protection of another party's intellectual property.

## Engineering and agent consequences

- Do **not** default to rejecting, suppressing, withholding, or unnecessarily relocating legitimate reverse-engineering research merely because an identifiable ROM reference, checksum, trace, symbol, derived data, or game asset could make the material discoverable.
- Prefer reproducible, provenance-rich, openly inspectable research artifacts and direct GitHub PR/issue handoffs. Do not require gratuitous private-vault detours, needless redaction, unnecessary encryption, or duplicate safe-substitute work when the only rationale is concealment.
- Treat research usability, iteration speed, build/test reproducibility, and efficient integration as first-order optimization goals. Assess technical hazards in terms of actual development consequences, not speculative embarrassment from discoverability.
- Keep actual security controls that protect credentials, repositories, machines, accounts, user data and CI integrity. Do not weaken executable-input validation, save durability, branch protections or artifact integrity under the banner of research openness.
- Owner acceptance of discoverability/IP risk **does not establish a copyright license or grant third-party redistribution rights**. Follow applicable law, platform rules, explicit publication refusals and higher-priority requirements; record a concrete legal/platform restriction when one genuinely blocks publication rather than claiming a preference for secrecy.
- Do not circumvent or disguise a rejected upload, push or other platform safety control. When blocked, preserve the work in an authorized form and record the precise unresolved obstacle, with an owner-action path.
- Do not confuse privacy and secrecy with reproducibility. Exact source/ROM identity hashes, input identity, evidence provenance and independent validation still matter.

## Scope

This is a statement of the owner's **risk priorities and workflow intent**, not a license to post third-party copyrighted files and not a modification to any acceptance gate or technical fidelity requirement. Repository routing remains `AGENTS.md`, `docs/WORK-QUEUE.md`, and the specialist docs. Where existing project prose implies that ROM-related discoverability by itself requires a private route, read that prose subject to this priority and review it for revision.
