## October 18: parallel fidelity work, independent acceptance, and recovery

The user directed a graphics/Windows integration lane toward source-authentic raster and presentation, a Windows test-harness lane toward working evidence rather than deceptive CI success, a gameplay lane toward original/native USA event coverage, and an independent QA lane toward the first accepted complete event. The independent QA request was explicit: inspect the existing Zoo/Bowl evidence, identify the exact blockers to moving beyond **0/45**, remove acceptance-side obstacles without weakening fidelity, and coordinate with CPU/APU owners instead of implementing gameplay in the QA lane.

Another conversation tasked an agent with validating frame evidence against latest `main`, active queue and QA authorities. The user repeatedly asked these agents to continue as far as practical, to work diligently and autonomously, and to recover stalled sessions. Those recovery messages were operational prompts to resume interrupted tools or context, **not** expressions of irritation or evidence that an agent had finished an assigned task.

A research/cleanup conversation asked when research factories should be rerun, whether those factories needed updating, and requested inspection of PRs and actual results from source-authentic producer experiments. A pointed instruction sought a **genuine executed read with its qualified opcode PC**, paired with original-side evidence and the corresponding raw-memory frame. This prioritizes causal, traceable evidence over broad speculative experiment volume.

An RF061-focused conversation was repeatedly resumed after interrupted sessions. A separate QA-review conversation ended with a request for a safe stopping point, fully current documentation and PRs. The visible prompt history records those objectives, **not** a guarantee of the resulting artifact state.
