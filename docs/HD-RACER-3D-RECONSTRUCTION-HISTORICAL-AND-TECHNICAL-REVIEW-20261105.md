**Review snapshot:** November 5, 2026, Calgary local time (some source actions are dated November 6 UTC). **Scope:** historical origin, original-game evidence, character/rig engineering, failed hypotheses, tooling, review and integration contracts, active work. **Status:** research and authoring; **no authenticated original 1994 3D production model, approved likeness, or shipping racer-model asset.** This report is a research index and synthesis, not a new art or release acceptance authority.

The **PR register at the end is a deliberately broad topic-retrieval inventory**; it does not imply that every matching PR is merged or independently qualified.

## 1. Executive assessment

The project has *effectively reconstructed crucial portions of the original 1994 2D sprite production and runtime presentation*, and built a credible experimental 2026 3D authoring and evaluation environment. It has **not** reconstructed the historic production mesh, authentic historical joint rig, scene camera, materials or source-to-3D animation state correspondence. The current 2026 model is a useful five-parent, 16-material **hypothesis**, not a recovered original.

This apparent paradox matters. Original ROM-identified sprite frames, real emulator sprites, and reconstructed original palette-index pixels can match exactly while infinitely many 3D models, cameras, part articulations and baked-lighting arrangements remain consistent with their small projected pictures. The line of effort has advanced from an attractive but rejected procedural 4× sprite treatment into **source-registered inverse graphics with honest, falsifiable measurements**. The tests increasingly show where an appealing static model fit comes at the price of wrong motion, hidden intersection geometry, insufficient camera constraints or leaked holdouts.

Strongest established results:
1. **1994 sprites:** original USA ROM contains a *24 phase × 64 angle-index* lookup, **1,536 distinct packed records**; authentic consecutive two-player Mesen observations 1601–1624 have uniquely matched **48/48** 30-tile payloads and all **196,608/196,608** original 4bpp index pixels. No modeling is required to obtain this exact *2D* result. See [ROM identity study](../tools/art/ORIGINAL-MESEN24-EXACT-ROM-POSE-IDENTITIES.md) and [original-ROM phase writer research](../tools/art/ORIGINAL-ROM-RIDER-GRAPHICS-LOOKUP.md).
2. **Authentic observation:** no-frame-skip Mesen captures provide two racers × 24 consecutive 64×64 original masks, **23 true adjacent temporal edges** per racer, **19 unique blue and 23 unique red** binary masks, SHA-bound to actual original ROM/PPU. Earlier source-to-screen original sprite pixel checks reproduced blue 323/323, red 327/327 at one countdown state; an additional 20 executed Mesen ticks yielded 12,937/12,937 mapped opaque sprite pixels after *observed* 2D reflection/colour changes. These are 2D causal and registration achievements, not historical geometry.
3. **Actual 2026 3D assets:** one original-inspired shared OpenSCAD rig is decomposed into **tire, rim/hub, fork/frame, pedals and saddle**, five articulated parent nodes and 16 material groups, exported as real GLB, inspected through the sole Pages 3D reviewer. Five named static poses, continuous authoring samples, separately reviewed coloured variants, and reversible physical geometry hypotheses exist. Draft [#2832](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/2832) remains *unapproved*; stacked experimental [#3124](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/3124) is **not on main as shipping geometry**.
4. **Source-verified discriminators:** exact source images, frozen 7:6 provisional camera, bounded 4× raster projection, one-vote-per-unique-source-shape scoring, chronological original 23-edge changed-pixel overlap, independent players, body-colour diagnostics, held-out families and tile-disjoint controls, actual mesh part visibility, geometric interference checks and SHA-pinned numeric receipts. Results routinely disagree across objectives; there is no defensible single leaderboard winner.
5. **Original official large-form references:** the already retained USA 1994 Nintendo/DMA manual has source-PDF-replayed p14, p15, p16 printed views and sparse 2D annotations, including exposed fork/hub/collar surfaces. The source is authentic printed period artwork. Whether a given print was rendered from the production *gameplay* mesh or a different model/camera, and the hidden 3D geometry behind occlusion, remain unknown.

**Current high-value next discriminator:** explain blue/red genuine temporal disagreement at original red **1605→1606** and **1621→1622** with source-index, paint, occlusion and real model-part event attribution, using the source-verified no-render edge-sensitivity tool before producing a fresh 3D geometry candidate. Preserve a fixed candidate across both racers and both wheel laws. Current *open* [#3195](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/3195) proposes durable retention of all 92 edge deltas and should be reconciled with [#3192](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/3192) rather than recreating a scorer.

## 2. What we are trying to make, and why

### What

Deliver a visually faithful, high-resolution, animated **reconstruction of the Uniracers/Unirally racer character** suitable as canonical art for the modern Windows x64 game: substantially the same distinctive original **rounded, expressive, pre-rendered unicycle**, consistent across perspectives, original player colourways, normal racing, jumps, tricks, recovery, contact and split-screen. Its source may be an editable 3D mesh/rig with reproducible baked pose output; a proven equivalent parametric representation is acceptable. The target is original *character identity and animated behaviour* at source/render quality, together with correct 64px game-space registration and high-density presentation.

The design specifics are visible in [the visual veto and likeness checklist](HD-RACER-CHARACTER-RECONSTRUCTION.md): broad curved black saddle functioning as a character's head, heavy rubber tyre, substantive metal rim/hub, rounded integrated coloured fork/body, recognisable crank/pedals and toy-like dimensional shading. The geometry must not degenerate into a thin stick, flat capsule or generic bicycle assembly. Source-history testimony identifies Martin Good as an original CG artist, with Robbie Graham describing detailed source renderings, variable saddle tilt/stretch and multiple pedal phases; see [original development history](original-development/DEVELOPER-TECHNICAL-HISTORY.md).

**Why:** the whole project's goal is a game that preserves original physics/gameplay while looking at home in a contemporary widescreen/4K context, with selectable **Original**, faithful **Remastered**, and freer **Reimagined** presentation classes. The original SNES sprite is tiny, pre-rendered from 3D and expressive despite drastic downsampling. Scaling those pixels has visual, depth and motion limitations, while independently painting hundreds of high-resolution sprites risks inconsistent character proportions and moving hardware. One coherent 3D/parametric model with reproducible camera, deformation, materials, original pose selection and palette variants is the best available route to a **consistent family of replacement images**, not a separate game engine.

Further goals include preservation and rigorous reverse engineering: independently understand and document recoverable original 1994 graphics/animation processes; make source references usable to technical and artistic collaborators; avoid fabrication of missing evidence; enable owner side-by-side inspection at actual game size; preserve Old/Original compatibility and runtime fallbacks. 3D art work is useful even when it never becomes a shipping model, because source analysis, reconstruction hypotheses and measured negative experiments remain reusable technical documentation.

### Boundaries

The original SNES guest remains authoritative for physics, controls, collision, contact, timing, pose/state selection, colour identity and gameplay outcomes. The modern host owns presentation only. The existing native presenter/OAM gates, semantic composition snapshot and 4× Original fallback must be reused, not replaced by a parallel runtime 3D simulation. Correct authoring exports must later pass independent normal/split-screen, layer/occlusion, footprint, temporal and physical 4K/Windows release tests. Original OAM/OBJ visibility and final composed pixels are different evidence. A model may be original-looking yet have wrong sprite coverage, or match 64px alpha while looking nothing like the original character at scale.

The owner's **October 10 artistic veto** of prior procedural high-density unicycles is binding for prospective art acceptance. The old 4× approvals, hashes, test runs and captured sequences retain their *historical technical* meaning, but old manifests claiming shipping art approved no longer settle likeness. Artistic sign-off, original-game fidelity, source-registration correctness, renderer integration, user-facing physical output and Windows release are separate gates.

## 4. What is missing or remains fundamentally unobserved

- **Original 1994 production 3D source:** no verified original editable mesh, source model coordinates, surface topology, subdivision, rig/bones, materials/texture maps, light setup, camera rig, compositing recipe, original-render frames or authoring project has been recovered. The ROM sprite frames are raster data, not a serialized original 3D asset. Potential artist archives and outreach are uncertain leads.
- **Historical geometry behind occlusion:** print p15 shows a dark exposed hub-like face, parts of fork and spokes but not a complete far side or source part-ownership graph; occluded contact parts, actual wheel depth and housing joint are underconstrained. A reconstructed closed 3D shell is a **proposal**.
- **Calibration:** no authenticated original camera pose, focal/projection law, scale or full print/game camera crosswalk. The frequently used **7:6** is the original display pixel-aspect correction, *not* proof of a 1994 artistic projection matrix. First-family \`9.5 px/model-unit\` and source-registered contact are 2026 experimental comparison controls, not physical calibrated source coordinates.
- **Original physical kinematics:** exact ROM 24×64 graphic lookup and executed WRAM phase are available; there is no proven physical transformation mapping a ROM phase to a 3D crank/wheel angle, individual saddle tilt, frame root lean, part joint degrees or true pedal-cycle count. Static 1994 2D source motion should not be presented as 3D pose labels.
- **Multiview correspondence and independent training evidence:** printed angles differ, sources may be art-directed, and 64px ROM sprite families share tiles/acting structure. Many nominal 2D holdouts are near duplicates. No prospective, camera-identified *historical 3D* train/validation corpus exists.
- **Clean material-to-pixel attribution:** 4bpp palette indexes and source red/blue colour masks are recovered, but assigning a particular coloured original pixel to an actual physical mesh parent or BRDF, especially through opaque/occluded regions, remains a hypothesis.
- **Artistic approval and production integration:** no new owner sign-off of 3D likeness, side/three-quarter character volume, dynamic action loop, game-scale split-screen appearance or final authored replacement. No completed path from approved GLB through faithful export to release-quality Windows presentation.
- **Camera/shape uniqueness:** even a perfect 2D fit is insufficient to recover unique physical geometry. A single unchanged 2026 saddle's unoccluded projected aspect ratio, viewed over a 37×29 coarse orthographic camera grid, met p14's visually uncertain 2D ratio from **508/1,073** directions and p15's from **188/1,073**. Those independently selectable cameras yield **95,504** compatible pairs with *zero mesh change*. This camera ambiguity is a *measured rejection of naive ratio-driven sculpting* rather than evidence of an original camera. [Source study #3177](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/3177).

These constraints mean the task is **inverse graphics with incomplete data, uncertain views and non-unique solutions**. Recover only what measurements justify; for unconstrained shape, make clearly documented artist choices and seek human likeness review.

## 5. Historical phases and decisions

### A. September 29–October 9: art direction, original evidence and safe presentation

[PR #14](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/14) proposed RetroArch/Slang/bsnes-hd/upscaler references but **closed unmerged**; [#28](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/28) merged a more durable art reference/HD registration plan. [#220](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/220) defined three presentation tiers. [#299](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/299) adopted smooth high-resolution source-consistent form, object-local baked lighting that flips with stock H/V presentation, and no automatic cartoon outline. Exact SNES OAM, frame identity, registered anchors, guest read-only bridges and host substitution advanced independently of modeling. A 64×64 sprite footprint and 4× pixel samples do not equal a full 4K native presentation.

The earlier procedural C++ high-density art was technically reproducible, and captured true authored moving frames through the Baldosa host. [#1072](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/1072), [#1080](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/1080) and the historical [#1301 media showcase](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/1301) document successful renderer/controls/asset provenance. [#1135](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/1135) found an **unsafe OAM-overlap-bypassing fixture**: its old "art proved" result was not production-safe source visibility. A positive 4× raster/hash result is therefore a test of a specific presentation contract, not a complete 3D artist's brief or safe split-screen release.

### B. October 10–18: owner likeness veto, reference-first reset and first shared rig

Review of recruitment footage triggered the explicit **owner veto of the procedural racers' visual likeness** on October 10. The project stopped promoting earlier hash/pose technical approvals to current Remastered art acceptance. [Character likeness brief](HD-RACER-CHARACTER-RECONSTRUCTION.md) established the large-form official references, original proportions/personality, five design components, side and expressive-pose two-view bakeoff, deliberate temporal review and human re-approval.

[PR #1312](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/1312) researched original artists, fan and professional collaborators, but did not secure a rig. Draft [#1826](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/1826) created **one OpenSCAD character source** with continuous frame/fork, rubber wheel, rim/hub/spokes, pedals and saddle; five static poses (upright/pedal/wheelie/stunt/bank), fixed side and three-quarter orthographic cameras, and separately scripted ride/stunt cycle motion. Actual rendered offline frames, four-edge crop guards, identical loop endpoints and multiple camera/geometry bug fixes were recorded in PR comments. The static fixed-view experiments revealed early stunt pivot mis-centering, clips at camera distances 11/12.5 under exaggerated mass, and misleading nominal 32/64px previews that downsampled a vast canvas rather than the character. These were repaired through bounds tests/common-family crop (see [#1840](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/1840)). None of those fixes verified original likeness or real ROM state-to-3D pose mapping. #1826 **closed without merging**; its source was later recovered, not discarded.

### C. October 29–November 1: revived real GLB, authoritative planning and first source fit

Open **draft [#2832](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/2832)** restored the same historical source and exposed a part-addressable authoring GLB (five parent parts, 16 material meshes). Initial ~23.5k–23.7k triangle exports evolved into actual material-separated five-pose GLBs around ~28.8k triangles. Five pose GLB snapshots and immutable per-attempt receipts were added. Independent PR review exposed that failed re-export could leave an old successful GLB masquerading as a fresh result, and that ambiguous second \`saddle\` module declarations or stray top-level OpenSCAD output could corrupt source-owned pivots/parts. The branch added source-parsed mounts, explicit module declaration census, and atomic success/failure pointers. Its focused draft CI **does not equal the full ready-head unit suite**.

Merged [#2927](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/2927) codified [the inverse-graphics spec](HD-RACER-INVERSE-GRAPHICS-SPEC.md) and [execution plan](HD-RACER-INVERSE-GRAPHICS-PLAN.md). A first [#2939](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/2939) pilot compared one original P1 frame1205 alpha against locally assembled real OpenSCAD geometry under one **provisional** camera: **IoU .5829**; camera scale-only search **.5957**; exploratory saddle span 1.4 **.6227 in-sample**. The large fitted number could not establish a correct saddle or generalize. [#2944](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/2944) independently inspected the **real** material-separated viewer GLBs; upright original-P1-1205 alpha IoU was exactly **.58288770**, with original 325, model 267 opaque logical pixels and 156 XOR residual pixels. Other five poses were deliberately compared **unpaired to the same** source frame: bank .60540541, pedal .584, wheelie .23760331, stunt .16500994. Bank's higher unpaired score does not mean the original frame is banked.

Merged [#2938](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/2938) established the **one** Pages 3D model reviewer under the Windows Visual Atlas. Original 1994 sprite rail is separately scrubbed from the five 2026 blockout poses; [#2948](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/2948) and [#2950](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/2950) made real GLB × independently SHA-hashed original shape comparisons and an optional fail-closed measurement sidecar; [#2957](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/2957) integrated it. An optional source-comparison "winner" is an IoU rank, **never an original pose identity/confidence**. Source/GLB staleness, mismatched PNG labels, invalid current pointers and accidental foreign-directory overwrites received dedicated unit regressions ([#2930](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/2930), [#2943](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/2943), [#2946](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/2946), [#2959](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/2959)).

### D. November 1–2: bigger original corpus, holdouts and source-qualified negative controls

The original ROM-composed source family was expanded to **27 original windows, 91 temporal observations, 59 representation IDs and 35 unique binary silhouettes**, with strict source hashes and frozen **binary-mask-disjoint** training and withheld source windows ([#2942](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/2942), [#2945](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/2945), [#2972](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/2972)). An early wheel-X 1.10× 2026-model geometry hypothesis was preferred by original first-family masks: .57255469→.59883507 IoU. A frozen 19-mask outside-first-family comparison favoured 1.10, but [#2982](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/2982) identified **16 nearly identical red leaning examples among 19**. At a *post-hoc* .90 IoU cluster threshold the 19 become **four correlated groups (16/1/1/1)**; balanced mean still improved .3624893→.3949460. [#3062](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/3062)/[#3063](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/3063) froze/audited six **tile-content-disjoint** source masks: 6/6 favour 1.10, .38543972→.41730258. That remains retrospective, source-2D geometry evidence with no recovered original 3D camera. #3063 also corrected a one-character source binary-mask SHA transcription bug without modifying underlying scores.

Fork thickness was a more cautionary result. First-family *in-sample* 2026 \`rig_frame_mass\` 1.0, 1.4, 2.0 produced .57255, .58272, .58540 IoU. Actual 19 outside-family masks favoured **1.4** (.38192089→.38824958), but **2.0 failed** (.38192089→.37325690) and 1.4 slightly worsened physical boundary Chamfer. Exact OpenSCAD CSG revealed fork/tire interpenetration volume **.00121168→.00560623→.01882075 model-unit³** at those same mass values, **4.63×/15.53×** baseline intersection ([#2963](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/2963), [#3007](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/3007)). A single alpha score can reward visibly wrong mechanics. Optional 1.4 source export [#3008](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/3008) and 1.1-wheel×1.4-fork matrix [#3009](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/3009) are *counterfactual studies*, not chosen canonical defaults. The combined candidate was reported ~.49816 versus .49030 wheel-only across 35 original masks in a preliminary fixed-camera study, subject to its stated hosted-evidence condition.

Colour and projected motion offered genuine additional constraints. Source first-family P1 red/P2 blue have different actual pixel/palette roles; original 2026 GLBs initially have the **same blue fork material** across viewer variants ([issue #2969](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/issues/2969), [#2965](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/2965), [#2967](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/2967)). Source **chromatic image-plane axes** spanned about **−29.19° to +43.12°** across original shapes, and withheld P1 lean examples were around +40° vs training near +1° ([#2973](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/2973), [#2984](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/2984)). One P2 painted-body mask leaked between otherwise full-alpha-disjoint training/withheld roles, demonstrating that different exact sprites may still repeat the same underlying "acting". Root-lean sweeps mapped image-plane colour-axis gaps, but did not prove 3D joint angles. Source-wheel-ring registration lowered absolute 35-mask IoU baseline from .46002071 to **.41237579–.42198267** with source centre ties, yet supported a fork/saddle hinge counterfactual; painted-axis-guided hinge achieved **.50134334–.51176089** in that alignment. These were non-blind 2026 source/camera hypotheses, and cannot be promoted from an impressive residual alone ([#3038](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/3038), [#3039](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/3039)).

Motion studies revealed why static multiframe fitting was inadequate. Real exported five-pose GLBs had **unstable topology**: only 9/16 material meshes shared identical local vertex arrays and 10/16 identical face indices; no equal world transforms across all poses, so direct vertex-index interpolation fails ([#2985](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/2985)). Continuous authored ride/stunt GLB outputs and original source temporal-edge tracking followed ([#2996](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/2996), [#2997](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/2997), [#3019](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/3019), [#3020](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/3020)). On genuine original 109 source edges, a static five-pose vocabulary initially explained **33/1,631** original changed pixels in pose-only comparisons (**2.02% recall**), and a 2026 sample phase has no authenticated 1994 phase correspondence. The five quarter-cycle GLBs were also *aliased* against a \`3*sin(720*t)\` authored saddle wobble: they all sampled its zero crossings. Eighth-cycle samples recovered its ±3° extremes, though 0/.5/1 were still equivalent states ([#3023](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/3023), [#3024](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/3024)). Eight equally spaced source spokes can further alias visual crank rotation every 45° ([#3033](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/3033)). Do not interpret a low-motion result from aliased frames as proof that the original source lacks the motion; do not fit a 3D drive to a static 2D source interval with unverifiable timing.

### E. November 2–4: authentic original emulator evidence supersedes synthetic pose assumptions

Some early "original" animation windows were **ROM-composed from authoritative data**, and some were spaced, not authentic consecutive PPU frames. [#3025](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/3025) and [#3026](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/3026) distinguished these evidence classes, and translation-only/translation-orbit controls [#3022](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/3022), [#3027](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/3027) were recognized as bounded 2D oracles rather than causal world motion. No arbitrary 2D sprite translation or rotation was allowed to invent an underlying physical 3D motion.

Actual original Mesen screenshots initially disagreed across repeated inputs even when OAM/VRAM/CGRAM agreed. [#3068](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/3068) **disabled emulator frame skipping** for source capture, yielding repeatable real screen pixels across independent replays. [#3065](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/3065), [#3066](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/3066), [#3074](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/3074) and [#3076](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/3076) independently decoded held OAM/high OAM, VRAM 4bpp and palette to actual source raster; one original countdown frame matched **323/323** blue and **327/327** red opaque screen pixels exactly. Later [#3080](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/3080)/[#3088](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/3088) registered **12,937/12,937** real observed original racer RGB pixels from 20 true original ticks after an observed screen reflection/red arithmetic adjustment. The causal scanline OAM writer/colour-math story is still narrower and open ([issue #3078](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/issues/3078)); 2D registration is already solved adequately for ongoing 3D work.

## 6. How experiments are designed and what each score means

The current method can be expressed as a bounded inverse-rendering optimization:

Given source-observed 2D images \`I_t\`, exact source ROM identities \`s_t\`, fixed visible orientation/position and a proposed geometric scene \`G\` with materials \`M\`, camera \`C\` and kinematics \`K(s_t)\`, project \`R(G,M,C,K(s_t))\` into source logical 64×64 physical-aspect space. Compare to \`I_t\` **without** quietly fitting a new independent 3D pose, camera, offset or wheel law per frame/player. Maintain a 2D-only geometric null and genuine physical 3D candidate arms separately.

Source classification must identify (a) ROM-composed registered bytes versus (b) real executed original PPU/state/framebuffer; (c) source OBJ plane versus final PPU-visible source pixels; (d) raw palette **index** versus final RGB/colour-math pixels; (e) official print illustration versus proven original gameplay 3D rendered view; (f) experimental 2026 GLB. The distinction determines what a test can conclude.

**Six locked source objectives per actual 2026 GLB arm:** for **each** original blue and red racer, retain (1) pooled IoU over all 24 chronological masks, (2) IoU with one vote per SHA-distinct original silhouette, and (3) changed-alpha XOR IoU over exactly **23 real consecutive original PPU edges**. Report source image-row TP/FP/FN and part-visibility accounting alongside them. A model dominates another *only* if no worse in all six and strictly better in ≥1. The Pareto **frontier is a diagnostic short list**, and its ranking must never train on different camera, physical wheel law, renderer or original-source hash sets. See [six-objective audit](../tools/art/REAL-GLB-ORIGINAL-MESEN24-SIX-OBJECTIVE-PARETO.md) and [#3150](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/3150)/[#3154](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/3154). An **unpaired** top GLB silhouette score has no authenticated 1994 pose-label confidence.

Useful secondary metrics:
- Pixel-level contact/lowest-alpha anchor, original 7:6 pixel aspect, bounded noncropped common camera, physical-pixel contour Chamfer, foreground/background/OBJ visibility, exact palette-index moments, red/blue chromatic axes, part-visibility contributions, original local 2D orbit/translation nulls and whole-scene mechanical CSG interference.
- Explicit temporal hold classifications, duplicate/near-duplicate source-group weighting, split leakage, held-out tile identity and re-derivation of all 4,096 indexed pixels per authentic source image.
- Exact GLB parent/mesh topology, 16 materials, source SHA, optional 3D part geometry change/revert assertions, watertightness/positive enclosed volume, actual fixed-camera browser before/after/restore raster.

The important failure classes are **overfit**, **wrong correspondence**, **camera–shape confounding**, **wrong or aliased temporal sampling**, **suspicious registration or cropping**, **hidden render versus physical source conflation**, and **metric optimization that damages plausible 3D assembly**.

## 9. What a plausible solution entails

The solution is not a hope that one hidden algorithm will invert the SNES sprite back into the lost exact mesh. A useful end state is an independently reproducible and artist-approved **single-source model** whose real geometry/camera/motion jointly explains the widest evidence available, with uncertainty explicitly recorded for anything source art cannot determine.

**Proposed evidence-gated order:**

1. **Stabilize source and compare representations correctly.** Reuse authentic 24×2 Mesen original frames, known 24×64 ROM IDs, actual source 4bpp and latest permanently retained GLB observations; avoid recompiling Mesen/re-exporting old arms when a named source-verified receipt answers the question. Do not confuse 2D source frame identity with inferred physical degrees.
2. **Solve current causal red-motion and apparent geometry-vs-occlusion problem.** Recompute no-render per-edge red vs blue 1605→1606 and 1621→1622 TP/FP/FN using source true 4bpp index, observed RGB/PPU and real part contribution. Exploit [#3192](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/3192) and current [#3195](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/pull/3195). Explain whether remaining differences require a physical part, a wrong pivot/drive law, source orientation/frame mismatch, material colour/lighting or hidden occlusion. Predeclare exactly one discriminating next hypothesis and its stop condition.
3. **Constrain big physical structure with genuinely complementary original views.** Use source-PDF-authenticated exposed p14/p15/p16 fork, saddle and hub outlines and 2D image-plane uncertainty. Obtain camera hypotheses *independently* before claiming geometry dimensions; record any 1994 artistic viewpoint mismatch or occlusion null. Retain the art-direction priority on saddle, tire, fork/crown and personality rather than chasing minute 64px IoU.
4. **Improve one canonical geometry/rig.** Keep physical wheel/fork clearance, 3D parent joint attachment and palette material identity. Refit only the part the source discriminator actually implicates; compare under one fixed source model/camera with unchanged controls. Preserve five parent/16 material source lineage or explicitly version the migration; do not blend five GLB snapshots by arbitrary vertex index.
5. **Solve time and pose only with source-owned evidence.** Relate genuine original ROM phase/orientation and CPU/WRAM writer observations to physically sensible wheel/crank motion and independent saddle/frame acting. Freeze a *shared* law across blue and red and actual 23-edge authentic motion. Use a real coherent temporal loop and novel disjoint original source families, not five attractive stills or synthetic quarter-phase sampling.
6. **Produce the owner-facing art bakeoff.** At least side + three-quarter + expressive turn/action, original high-detail authenticated reference, original source gameplay, vetoed 4× technical art and new model render side-by-side. Review normal 32/64px game scale, both red/blue material variants, full 4×/4K authoring scale, 1P/2P and temporal clip with no per-pose camera cheats. Record specific likeness approval or rejection independently of geometry/CI metrics.
7. **Only then integrate source-generated art.** Use the existing semantic identity, original cadence, contact/pivot, OAM source visibility and fail-closed runtime fallback. Compare Original/Remastered in real Windows output and review under existing release and QA governance. Genuine source-authoritative 3D art approval never follows from green CI alone.

**Avoid:** a new viewer, another rig fork, a second ROM parser, per-frame neural redraws, unweighted static IoU promotion, overfitting blue while changing red's wheel law, gratuitous scheduled Windows rebuilds, or speculative camera fitting that retroactively turns the original print into a "recovered" 3D view.

## 11. Open questions and decisions that remain

1. Can original high-detail CGI source artwork be acquired with reliable provenance and permission, or can an original developer/artist confirm the actual shape/lighting/motion? No actual 1994 mesh yet.
2. Which 3D parts or material/occlusion conditions are responsible for original red temporal mismatches when the shell improves both static silhouettes?
3. Is any specific hub cover mechanically attached to the wheel, axle or fork in the 1994 gameplay source scene? Current axle-lock model counterfactual did not improve true motion jointly.
4. Are the original print p14/p15/p16 images suitable for *shared* camera/geometry constraints, or are the artist scenes materially different? The existing p14/p15 saddle projected-aspect ambiguity argues strongly for more constraints.
5. Which physical degrees of freedom, if any, map faithfully from the original 24×64 **graphic** grid and writer values, and can one blue/red model reproduce consecutive original motion rather than static mask averages?
6. Can a source-calibrated 3D model simultaneously preserve curved saddle/head personality, meaningful fork/wheel mass, hidden-wheel thickness and original-game small-scale readability across truly independent views?
7. Which actual material assignments match P1 red/P2 blue source artwork with identical underlying geometry, and what part of 4bpp colour change comes from light/baked rendering rather than material?
8. What specific human visual reference packet and explicit approval would justify promoting the current draft art after the owner's earlier rejection, without promoting stills on CI alone?

**Bottom line:** use the recovered original **2D production truth** as an unusually strong experimental oracle and treat the current shared 2026 3D model as one carefully instrumented, reversible physical hypothesis. The engineering tools and evidence hygiene are strong; the remaining challenge is *original character identity, physical shape, source camera and continuous animation correspondence* under deep underdetermination. The closest route to an authentic remaster is disciplined source/camera/geometry separation, more independent high-detail reference constraints, physical-rig coherence and explicit artistic review.
