## Media and attachment checklist

For Blender Artists, include a rendered side/front/three-quarter comparison grounded in the latest 2026 model and available source images, clearly labeled experimental. For lettering posts, show side-by-side original traced English and experimental regional SVG previews, and keep each script's language validation separate. For nostalgic/gameplay/tester posts, provide a genuine Windows gameplay comparison clip bound to source and verified capture provenance. Do not label decorative promo art, unapproved 3D renders or an old candidate as current playable evidence.

Keep attachments modest and within forum rules. Preserve submission permissions and rights metadata; do not distribute original ROMs, private saved data, copyrighted bulk exports, or non-redistributable materials in outreach. Approved landscape promo art uses the existing canonical PNGs; new portrait promotional video backgrounds must follow assets/promo/canonical/README.md.
