Proposed title: [Volunteer / unpaid] Reconstruct an articulated racing unicycle from SNES sprites

## Proposed post

I'm coordinating an unofficial recreation of the 1994 SNES game Uniracers / Unirally and looking for a Blender artist interested in reconstructing its expressive, racing unicycle as a detailed 3D model.

**Project at a glance:** [Uniracers / Unirally volunteer opportunities](https://gamesbyian.github.io/UR-Recomp/contributors/). A short, mobile-friendly explanation of our progress, the ways to help and how to get involved.

There is already an experimental five-part 2026 model and a substantial library of sprite/reconstruction evidence, so this is a real, bounded art challenge rather than a concept without references. The current model isn't a recovered 1994 original or a finished character. It needs a trained eye for side/front/three-quarter likeness, coherent wheel and fork geometry, saddle and axle refinement, and credible articulated motion for pedalling, leaning, jumping and tricks.

**See the actual visual reference set:** [3× title-screen racer and three contour studies](https://gamesbyian.github.io/UR-Recomp/contributors/3d/). The full-size [title-screen composition](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/blob/main/media/showcase/art-reconstruction/2026-11-09/title-screen-racer/title-screen-racer-3x-upscale.png), [wide outline study](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/blob/main/media/showcase/art-reconstruction/2026-11-09/title-screen-racer/racer-contour-overlay-wide.png), and [axle/seatpost detail](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/blob/main/media/showcase/art-reconstruction/2026-11-09/title-screen-racer/racer-contour-overlay-axle-detail.png) are supplied specifically to help interpret the character's shapes. The outlines are artistic interpretation, not verified original 3D mesh seams.

For deeper technical detail: [current artist brief](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/blob/main/docs/HD-RACER-CHARACTER-RECONSTRUCTION.md), [art direction](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/blob/main/docs/HD-ART-DIRECTION.md), [experimental model viewer source](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/blob/main/tools/art/model-review/visual-review.html), [approved promotional background (not a gameplay screenshot)](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/blob/main/assets/promo/canonical/blueprint-16x9-logo-left.png).

This is a **voluntary, unpaid fan project**, with named credit and portfolio use where rights permit. A first contribution could simply be a critique and one refined model component, not a permanent commitment. If interested, reply here with a relevant example and the part you'd most like to work on.

I'm also actively interested in coordinating with other Uniracers / Unirally remakes, recompilations, preservation and art projects. I'm familiar with the [Baldosa](https://github.com/baldosa/uniracers-recomp) and [malmazuke](https://github.com/malmazuke/unirally-reconstruction) projects, and would welcome contact from other teams to share findings or avoid duplicating work. I'm not claiming any current partnership with them.

For 3D artists, start with the [live 3D reviewer, likeness comparisons and downloadable OpenSCAD source](https://gamesbyian.github.io/UR-Recomp/contributors/3d/). Current public GLB availability is manifest-dependent; the editable source model is always provided.
