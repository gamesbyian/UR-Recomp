# Russian Unirally wordmark: Юниралли

**Status:** Original Cyrillic wordmark proposal, pending owner artistic review.  
**Date:** 2026-11-08.

The user supplied the historically attested Russian rendering **Юниралли**, romanized *Yuniralli*. This artwork uses that exact eight-character Cyrillic sequence; this change does not independently verify historical usage or imply that the original SNES game had an official Russian release.

Vector asset: [`assets/branding/unirally-russian-cyrillic-wordmark.svg`](../../assets/branding/unirally-russian-cyrillic-wordmark.svg).

The illustration contains eight individually editable custom-drawn Cyrillic characters: **Ю н и р а л л и**, composed from 20 cubic Bézier centerlines with rounded endpoints and organic widths. The initial uppercase Ю and the two distinct lowercase л letters are preserved. No operating-system font, embedded raster, or external asset is needed to render the logo.

**Visual treatment:** yellow `#ffea00`, black `#090409`, diagonally displaced (+2,+2) black backing expanded 8 units beyond each base stroke width, and centered 2.4-unit black keyline, matching the Japanese and English wordmark recipes. The Russian characters remain independent silhouettes with no forced reuse of Latin letter paths; the Cyrillic script requires its own shapes, but the identity is shared through line weight, curves, palette and border.

The English Uniracers / Unirally SVGs, six-glyph shared `unira` master, and Japanese ユニレーサーズ wordmark are **unchanged**. Before considering this an approved localization logo, inspect at large and small sizes for Cyrillic readability, letter spacing and closer resemblance to the English logo's whimsical curvature. This is an art asset, not ROM research, gameplay evidence or release qualification.
