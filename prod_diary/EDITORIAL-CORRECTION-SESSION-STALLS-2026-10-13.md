# Editorial correction: session-stall reports

The user clarified that reports such as “Session stalled, please recover and continue” are operational instructions to diagnose, recover and resume work. They do **not** express impatience, frustration, or criticism of the agent. Earlier production-diary language attributing such emotions to these reports is an unsupported inference and should be disregarded.

This correction applies to the October 10 and October 11–13 production entries and to the corresponding Cajun continuous diary, wherever stalled-session reports were described as evidence of irritation, impatience or dissatisfaction. These reports should be understood as useful status signals within the project's autonomous-development workflow.

Emotional analysis in the diary must be grounded in explicit statements, not inferred from troubleshooting requests. This erratum takes precedence over conflicting characterizations in historical entries.
