Volunteer art · Seeking human-made 3D models

Help find the right racer model.

The project is now seeking an artist-created 3D racing unicycle model, whether an existing model its creator can share or a new volunteer-made one. Procedural reconstruction work is paused while those possibilities are explored. The experimental 2026 rig and original artwork below remain reference material, not a required starting point.

What the original title-screen racer looks like

These four user-supplied references show a 3× upscaled title-screen image and interpretive outline studies. The gallery displays lightweight, non-generatively downscaled previews; tap any image to inspect its unchanged full-resolution original in GitHub. Use them to judge proportions, contour transitions, the saddle, frame, collar, wheel and pedal separation. The line work is an artist's interpretation, not recovered 3D topology.

Reference-only title-screen imagery. Compare against the current experimental 2026 mesh below; the original 1994 3D geometry and joint structure have not been recovered.

Additional wheel and fork details · box-art studies

Two more artist-provided outline studies use crops of the original box artwork. Compare their tire sidewalls, rim and spoke shapes, hub, fork contours, crank and pedal details against the title-screen references above. Highlights and drawn separation lines are illustrative, not verified 3D mesh structure.

The two source crops are small enough to be served unchanged. Their captions identify the source editions from the original filenames, rather than claiming the drawn strokes prove the underlying 1994 model topology.

Have a model, or interested in making one?

If you've made a suitable unicycle model, know someone who has, or would like to create one by hand, please get in touch. Share images or a portfolio first; any source/model reuse must come with clear permission from its creator. The old procedural mesh is only an optional reference, and no new fitting work is currently assigned. Contributions are unpaid and optional. No GitHub account is required.
