# Physical wheel support reconstruction experiment: 2026 provisional eight-spoke GLB

**Nonshipping 2026 source-model geometry only. No recovered original 1994 model, camera, spoke count or materials.**

The original official Nintendo/DMA 1994 printed manual p15 presents a large oblique/underside wheel with conspicuously **broad metallic support facets** and a substantial hub/crank assembly. The printed artwork may not share the original gameplay's lost source mesh or render camera. The current single 2026 GLB in draft #2832 instead contains **eight narrow, largely round radial spoke supports**, authored in OpenSCAD with fixed orientation `11° + i × 45°`; no original part-by-part 3D likeness acceptance exists. Wheel supports are exactly one source material/mesh, `rim_and_hub__material_2`, 1,788 vertices/3,572 triangles in the exact authenticated 2026 upright GLB from original [real-model qualification #42099979791](https://github.com/gamesbyian/UR-Recomp/actions/runs/42099979791), SHA-256 `d83a39b805107eb309d3f56db8d5aeb77f435038170005fd5d15c0c45ed4c94a`.

A new *one-candidate* reversible source geometry experiment makes a **blade-like, wheel-plane-wider spoke profile** without inventing spoke count, changing full-wheel radius, adding/material swapping meshes, moving its original rig nodes or changing any other part:

- For each original spoke vertex use its authored polar angle `theta = atan2(x,z)` and radius `r=hypot(x,z)` in the wheel X/Z plane. Select the nearest of the eight unchanged spokes centred at `11° + 45°×k` and compute shortest angular displacement `delta` from that axis.
- The fixed local width multiplier is **2.0 at r≥0.72** 2026 model units, transitioning quadratically from **1.0 for r≤0.12**. New angle `theta'=axis+delta×(1+min(1,max(0,(r−.12)/.60))²)`; new X/Z are `r*sin(theta')`/`r*cos(theta')`. Y is untouched. The existing hub-connected spoke root remains byte-equivalent; the outer radius of every vertex stays fixed. The shape becomes fuller near the rim, consistent with one *qualitative* aspect of the manual's oblique printed supports.
- No other triangle geometry, vertex colour, original five-parent pose, frame, saddle, tire, rim-face, original eight spoke count, hub or pedal mesh is modified. Exact exception-safe original spoke-vertex rollback is mandatory.
- This is a **source-inspired proposed 2026 sculpt**, not a physically measured 1994 blade-width factor. The official print crop p15 still needs an actual exact-source PDF pixel replay and 2D landmark annotation under merged #3166/#3170. Do not treat visually broad shading in the manual as a proven original model part, 3D triangle or count. Signed source Mesen24 binary silhouette / real chronology scores have not been computed for this shape; the previous #3163 crown/saddle score results are **not transferred** to spokes.
