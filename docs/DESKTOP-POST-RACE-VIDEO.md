# Desktop post-race video: Save / Discard

**Status: explicitly approved product intent; LIVE CAPTURE FIRST, NOT IMPLEMENTED.** Scope: **Windows x64, macOS, and Linux desktop Modern mode** after a genuine completed race. Windows is the initial delivery owner; macOS/Linux gain the same product contract when desktop hosts are activated. Web and consoles have no feature promise. This document owns this video-specific policy; see [COMPLETED-RUN-RECORDS.md](COMPLETED-RUN-RECORDS.md), [COMPLETED-RUN-BROWSER.md](COMPLETED-RUN-BROWSER.md), [MODERN-RESULTS-NAVIGATION.md](MODERN-RESULTS-NAVIGATION.md) and [PLATFORM-TARGETS.md](PLATFORM-TARGETS.md) for existing authoritative run, replay, result and platform seams.

## Decisive user requirement: record what actually happened

**2026-10-10 correction to the original replay-first proposal:** The purpose of Save Race Video is to preserve the **actual run the player witnessed**, including **glitches, nondeterministic visual oddities, transient timing/audio problems, unexpected objects and rendering defects**. A deterministic .urrun reproduction might erase precisely the anomaly the player wants to show. Therefore **live audiovisual capture of the real presented race is the required primary path** on Windows/macOS/Linux; it is not an optional add-on or a fallback after replay export. This requirement supersedes every older "prefer replay-to-video" statement in this document or other plans. There is **no prerequisite to prove replay determinism or to have a valid .urrun** before offering live video for a completed event.

Replay-derived export may exist later under the explicit, different name **Render Replay to Video** in Records; it must be identified as a reconstruction, not a witnessed recording, whether or not the common simulation reproduces the outcome.

An in-process capture tap can preserve the app's **final composed frame submitted for presentation**, host overlays/display treatments and the mixed audio sent to its device. It cannot independently prove defects that occur **downstream** of that tap (display cable/monitor, OS compositor, graphics driver present failure after the tap, or audio hardware) or anomalies lost by the encoder. Do not promise a literal screen-photograph oracle. For source anomalies, capture as late as practically possible in the game presentation pipeline without introducing capture-caused stalls; retain timing/encoder-gap evidence to distinguish capture faults from original game glitches.

## Player-facing decision (the feature being requested)

On completing an eligible race, **preserve the original results ritual**, then show a nonintrusive Modern controller/keyboard-navigable **Save Race Video / Discard Video** decision. Video is a normal, locally playable and shareable movie, preferably **MP4 (H.264 video + AAC audio)**. The player chooses; do not auto-retain huge files without opt-in. The decision can be dismissed and should not replace Next Event, Retry, Tour/Track Select, Records, or the stock result. Prefer a compact card/row with explicit status, duration and estimated size, rather than blocking the result screen. The keyboard and physical controller use the **existing** semantic Modern navigation map. Authentic mode never shows or runs this feature.

**Words must follow durable facts:**

- **Save Race Video** requests export of the just-completed attempt. If an exact complete encoded movie already exists and can be published, display **Video saved** only *after* completed output has been validated and atomically committed. Offer **Open Folder / Show File** and optional **Play Video** only when an actual readable file exists.
- If the **already captured** recording is finalizing/remuxing, show **Finalizing video** or **Saving video** immediately, followed by **Video saved** only after file verification and successful publication. A request may complete asynchronously while the user continues navigating; persist the actual pending job/source and its result, never a fake queued success. A distinct, future **Render Replay to Video** action may report **Replay render queued** when applicable.
- **Discard Video** discards the **already live-captured encoded candidate** and promptly releases only its owned bounded RAM buffer or temporary compressed spool. It **does not delete** canonical .urrun, .urghost, .urmatch, scores, PB, progression, achievements, controller history, or other authoritative records. Discard never starts a replay export.
- Do not present a successful Save/Discard offer if **live capture did not start at the required race-entry boundary** or lost any required frame/audio span. Use a truthful **Video unavailable: <short reason>** (or an explicit partial-recording recovery action, never labelled full race). Replay availability does not silently turn an incomplete recording into a reconstructed replacement. A cropped tail of a long race is never misrepresented as a **full-race** video; explicit **Save Last N Seconds** may be a separately named future feature.
- The decision belongs to the exact *race attempt and active-profile snapshot* that settled at results, not a later course/profile or a currently mounted replay. Resume/Restart/Next Event should not silently discard a requested durable export.

**User options (design, not yet persisted):** **Ask After Race (Live Capture)**, Off, and optionally Auto-save, explicitly disclosing that Ask records a **temporary compressed candidate during each eligible race** so the player can decide afterward. Offer the toggle at first-run/onboarding or in Options **before race entry**; no retroactive saving of unrecorded frames. Choose capture quality (initial pilot 1080p, H.264/AAC, source presentation cadence where supported), video directory, cap/quota and later 720p/4K where qualified. Make record-on-start state visible but unobtrusive. Only enable automatic live capture after transparent opt-in/capability checks, with a way to turn it off. No host-state field until a backend plus migration/tests exist.

## Key distinction: a tiny deterministic run cannot substitute for a witnessed video

A .urrun is a versioned compact **input/state provenance** artifact for re-driving the authoritative game; it is not encoded frame/audio output. The current production recording eligibility is **narrower than all events**: see [COMPLETED-RUN-RECORDS.md](COMPLETED-RUN-RECORDS.md) for supported 1P timed Race cases, unfinished Circuit/Stunt/2P/VS admission, and canonical control stream details. [COMPLETED-RUN-BROWSER.md](COMPLETED-RUN-BROWSER.md) can stage and re-drive valid records, but its current acceptance explicitly notes that even compatible replay traces may differ in presentation RNG/pose. **A replay's timing/inputs matching is not proof that every pixel, sound effect or music sample is identical to what was played.**

**Primary implementation and optional later export are distinct products:**

| Candidate | During race | At Save | Strength / decisive caveat |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Live encoded capture: required primary path** | Tap the actual composed frame and mixed audio as presented in this attempt; hardware-preferred H.264, bounded input/packet queue and finite compressed temporary spool or size-limited RAM. Start at race entry and stop after the approved finish/results sequence. | Finalize/stream-copy already compressed media, verify then atomically publish a real MP4. No re-simulation. | **Preserves observed nondeterministic defects and presentation quirks present at the tap**, independent of .urrun replay eligibility; bounded encoding/RAM plus finite low-rate sequential temp writes (or explicitly bounded compressed RAM). |
| **B. Render Replay to Video: optional later Records action** | No live recording required for this separate action; use existing compact run inputs only when compatible. | Replay/re-render and encode to a *recreated* movie with honest status. | Cheap during original gameplay, but replay may omit or change exactly the bug the player saw. **Never label it Save Race Video; never fall back to it silently when live capture fails.** |
| **C. Raw frames in memory / PNG dump / recording uncompressed video** | Massive raw raster buffers and/or heavy writes | Encode from raw later | **Rejected** for automatic gameplay capture. A 1920×1080×4-byte frame alone is ~8 MiB; 60 such frames/s is ~475 MiB/s uncompressed before audio. Do not use this as a default architecture. |

**Product priority:** Implement **A (live capture)** first and gate availability by a successful active recording of the complete attempt, **not** by `.urrun` support or deterministic replay equivalence. Ask After Race is explicitly an **opt-in transient live-recording mode** beginning before the first race frame, not an after-the-fact capture request. If capture is disabled/unavailable at start or fails, do not pretend to save the last race. Only a distinct later Records action may use B.

### Near-instant save without abusing RAM or a drive

For primary live capture A, use the smallest pragmatic architecture **verified on the actual graphics/audio host**:

1. Capture the **actual final Modern composed output submitted for the displayed race** (not a fresh offline render), after view, original pixel-aspect mapping, animated sprites, HUD, ghost and enabled display treatments. Include visible renderer glitches/overlay oddities. Tap the **mixed digital game audio actually submitted for playback**, before the device. Start at the owned race-entry boundary and include the genuine finish/results reveal (but not the Save/Discard UI). Preserve wall/presentation timestamps; if 120/144 Hz frames exceed the encoder's negotiated capture cadence, report skipped frames honestly, offer a correctly labelled rate option, and never synthesize motion or replace anomalies with deterministic replay. An async GPU staging/zero-copy path is preferable to synchronous framebuffer readback where supported; measure this rather than assume it is free.
2. Prefer hardware-backed H.264 per OS where available (Windows Media Foundation hardware encoder, macOS VideoToolbox, Linux VA-API/driver encoder or FFmpeg/GStreamer adapter). Capability-probe encoding + container **before** enabling A; a system API’s mere presence or a listed codec is not a passed working hardware encoder. Safe fallback or explicit **unsupported** is better than making a weak computer drop frames. Treat any FFmpeg/libavcodec distribution as a separately audited licence and codec-patent issue.
3. Use **compressed encoded packets**, not a giant RGBA ring. **Preferred full-race default:** a strictly **quota-limited sequential temporary compressed spool** (one stream, not one PNG per frame), with a small bounded in-flight queue. At 4 Mbit/s video + 160 kbit/s audio, ~31 MB/minute of disk traffic is an illustrative budget, much less than raw framebuffer recording but **not zero**. Bounded compressed RAM-only capture is an optional short-race optimization when the configured entire-race budget is provably sufficient; an overwriting OBS-style rolling buffer cannot promise the beginning of an unknown-length race. Explicitly disclose staging and the disk/RAM cap when enabling Ask After Race; stop retaining a full-race offer rather than silently overwriting the start or spilling to unlimited disk.
4. Illustrative **planning budgets, NOT accepted defaults**: 4 Mbit/s video + 160 kbit/s audio ≈ **31.2 MB per minute**, ≈ **93.6 MB in three minutes**; 64 MiB of encoded RAM fits only ≈ **129 seconds** at that rate, without queue/container overhead. Measure actual bitrate peaks and output size using representative motion/2P. If a proposed cap cannot cover the full race, declare full-race instant capture unavailable unless a permitted spill/spool is already active.
5. Keep GPU staging, encoder input and packet queues bounded by **bytes and frames**, measuring peak RSS, VRAM staging, readback and encoder utilization. Backpressure must not block gameplay or skew input/audio. Record precise skipped/dropped/discontinuous frames, audio gaps, and present→encoded timestamp mapping in a small diagnostics sidecar. If this happens, retain the truthful **Capture incomplete** diagnosis (optionally offer an explicitly labelled partial file) rather than silently smoothing/retiming, fabricating a perfect 60-Hz race, or claiming a complete anomaly witness. Distinguish genuine in-game frame pauses/duplicates from capture-pipeline losses. Recording itself can perturb performance; compare enabled/disabled frame pacing in tests.
6. Use a crash-aware temp container or fragments (e.g. Matroska or correctly fragmented MP4, after compatibility testing); avoid an extra full-file \`+faststart\` rewrite when a normal local MP4 is already usable. A fast rename of a **fully finalized same-volume** file can publish almost instantly; when a remux is necessary, perform **stream copy** (no decode/re-encode) and measure latency/extra I/O. For multi-segment encoding, prove boundary keyframes, A/V continuity and full attempt coverage, not only that fragments each play.
7. Save with **unique attempt-derived filenames** (collision safe), write into a restricted staging path under the existing profile/user-data policy, validate container duration, track presence, start/end coverage, decode probe, and source/run identity before final publication. Avoid destructive overwrite; use an atomic, no-replace final commit on each OS. Persist a durable ready status separately from mere intent; on restart reconcile incomplete/orphan stages safely. Discard cleans only its own temporary video objects, not the selected canonical run or unrelated user media.
8. Benchmark first save confirmation latency and end-to-end ready time separately: **click→durably queued**, **click→file playable**, and **click→user-facing confirmation**. A rapid “queued” response is good UX but must not masquerade as a completed export.

### Initial performance targets (proposed, not measurements)

For a bounded Windows 1080p/60 H.264/AAC live-capture pilot, set explicit *performance acceptance targets* rather than treating “fast” as subjective: **p95 click→save/finalizing acknowledgment under 250 ms** for an already encoded and complete race; **p95 click→playable finalized MP4 under 2 seconds** for the live-encoded full-race candidate on a qualified reference machine; **no video-induced guest-frame delay, audio underrun, controller-input change or race-result divergence**; and a preliminary **64 MiB additional host RAM ceiling** outside explicitly disclosed driver/GPU surfaces. Test and revise the numbers only with reported device/codec evidence, never by silently lifting memory caps. If 2 seconds is impossible for a selected file system/container, expose honest **Finalizing** instead of incorrectly reporting success. Later replay-derived export is independent of these goals and must never masquerade as a capture of the original attempt.

For live capture, compare **total drive bytes** and measured **sustained/peak write throughput**, not file-count alone. Target one sequential compressed write stream rather than per-frame PNG files, and reserve quota/headroom before recording. On platforms or disks where even low-rate video writes cause real gameplay stalls, disable **live capture** for that configuration with an explicit warning. An optional later reconstructed replay export is not an equivalent replacement, and no video feature may change the guest clock.

## Technical options, measured evidence and initial choice

- **Windows:** Microsoft [Media Foundation H.264 encoder](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-ca/windows/win32/medfound/h-264-video-encoder) + [Sink Writer](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/medfound/sink-writer). [Hardware transform activation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/medfound/mf-readwrite-enable-hardware-transforms) explicitly needs enablement and runtime probing; Media Foundation may otherwise select software. If using current SDL3/OpenGL render targets, measure whether GPU readback + colour-space conversion outweighs accelerated encoder gains.
- **macOS:** [VideoToolbox compression sessions](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/videotoolbox/vtcompressionsession-api-collection) and [AVAssetWriter fragmented file support](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/avfoundation/avassetwriter/moviefragmentinterval). AVFoundation documents short initial fragment and longer subsequent intervals, but only use fragmentation once actual playable-file/latency tests pass on the intended deployment target including any legacy macOS support.
- **Linux:** FFmpeg/libavcodec hardware encoders (notably VA-API, NVIDIA NVENC or Intel QSV when available); a [GStreamer splitmuxsink](https://gstreamer.freedesktop.org/documentation/multifile/splitmuxsink.html) can asynchronously finalize segments and bound retained temp files. [GStreamer appsrc/appsink](https://gstreamer.freedesktop.org/documentation/app/appsrc.html) has bounded queue parameters. Make driver/codec availability explicit; no distro-wide hardware assumption.
- **Existing strong precedent:** [OBS encoded-packet replay buffer](https://github.com/obsproject/obs-studio/blob/master/plugins/obs-ffmpeg/obs-ffmpeg-mux.c) demonstrates keeping compressed packets and writing a separate output asynchronously. This is conceptual code precedent, **not** a claim that OBS as a whole should be linked or vendored.
- **Containerization:** [FFmpeg streamcopy](https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg.html) can change a container without re-encoding, but source compatibility matters. [FFmpeg fragmented MP4 documentation](https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-formats.html) explains the crash-resistance/compatibility trade-off. [FFmpeg licensing](https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html) requires review of the exact library/build/package configuration; do not copy an unspecified system binary into the island.

**Acquisition decision:** FFmpeg already appears among UR-Recomp's system inspection/media tools, not as a complete bundled encoder dependency; its presence is not proof of portable Windows/macOS end-user availability. No new executable/video framework should be vendored from these research links until a tiny exact-renderer/audio **capture → encode → remux → probe** prototype shows the selected solution is worth its runtime, licence, island and packaging cost. Prefer adapting current renderer/audio hooks over a second game host or a separate gameplay recorder.

## Ownership, job persistence and state contract

- This is a **Modern host feature** layered on the existing result context and per-profile presentation/user-data root, **independent of whether a .urrun exists**. The game remains the single authoritative simulation. Do **not** change \`.urrun\` codec or make video data part of SRAM, progression, PB, deterministic-replay comparability or required Records indexing.
- Create one host-neutral \`RaceVideoOffer\` semantic projection: validated just-finished attempt key, profile/ROM/build/course/mode, available backend, status, quality, proposed path and any full-attempt integrity guard. An ID must be bound to the result attempt at finish, not to mutable “most recent run”. A given result shows one offer and does not issue duplicate capture/export requests on repeated host frames.
- Host backend handles \`Unavailable → Eligible → Offered → Discarded\` or \`Requested → Queued/Finalizing → Ready/Failed\` as *conceptual states*. Keep renderer/OS/video muxer details below a narrow platform interface. Never persist video settings/jobs in \`HostProductState\` v6 before a real concrete consumer, migration and safe rollback. Use existing profile user-data-root policy; media directory and quotas should not pollute existing run-catalog semantics.
- The live candidate must bind the **original captured attempt**, media start/end timestamps, observed result snapshot and active profile/build identity, not the result of a later replay. Finalization jobs must be restart-safe/idempotent and may keep only their own compressed staging objects. No second simulation is started to service Save Race Video. Future *Render Replay to Video* jobs hold a separately validated immutable .urrun and must not compete with active gameplay.
- UI shall keep honest status and cancellation/retry. Do not write “Video Saved” before the asset is complete. On app exit, user-requested work must have a truthful persisted/paused status; incomplete clips cannot become visible as successful. Out-of-space, unwritable target, encoder crash, lost source record, source mismatch or media-probe failure leave the canonical game/run intact.
- Once ready, optionally show the saved **actual-live video** in Records alongside its attempt metadata, without requiring a matching .urrun. A separate later **Render Replay to Video** action in Records may create a plainly labelled recreated video for supported .urrun inputs. Never infer that a replay export was captured live or overwrite the live witness, and never change ghost/PB sorting.

## Sequencing and release criteria

1. **Phase 0, narrow research probe (Windows only):** same source captured frames/audio → Media Foundation and/or existing FFmpeg host path; measure encoding CPU/GPU use, raw readback cost, bounded memory, sequential disk volume, audio sync and end-of-race finalization/remux time. Test an actual run with motion and a 2P presentation sample. Record source/build hashes and executable output. No default CI or live recording.
2. **Phase 1, live capture witness acceptance:** inject a known one-frame visual anomaly and a bounded audio glitch into the already-running host presentation **without mutating guest state**; capture an actual complete race and prove both are observable in the encoded file at the expected timestamps, including the original result sequence. Repeat with a genuine nondeterministic/presentation timing perturbation and prove the recording is not replaced by a normal-looking deterministic replay. Validate the tap is post-composition and frame time is preserved; inspect any aliasing or encoder omissions. Compare guest results/input hashes and gameplay frame pacing against no-recording control.
3. **Phase 2, guarded post-result UI & local publication:** only offer a full-race Save/Discard if this precise attempt has an intact live candidate. Test keyboard/pad, settled results, prompt dismissal, cleanup, reruns, simultaneous sessions/processes, profile changes, capture start/end boundaries, 1×–4×/4:3/16:9/2P, resolution changes, Original/HD display, incomplete recordings and limited-disk failure. Live capture should work independent of .urrun eligibility where observed race/result gating is proven. Never alter guest state, timer, result, audio submission or prior video on failed save.
4. **Phase 3, opt-in player rollout:** expose Ask After Race with a clear temporary-recording/cap disclosure only once bounded hardware encode/physical-host tests prove no unacceptable gameplay pacing/audio damage, no hidden RAM growth, controlled sequential I/O and fast *actual playable* confirmation. Hardware encode unavailability degrades to explicit unsupported or tested safe software backend, **never** to replay substitution without telling the user.
5. **Phase 4, macOS/Linux:** reuse the common host-neutral policy and fixtures, bring up platform encoders with exact capability/packaging tests before advertising. macOS 10.13 compatibility is best-effort, not a reason to constrain current Windows.
6. **Later:** separately labelled Records **Render Replay to Video** for compatible inputs, metadata/filename templates, higher resolutions, trim/share convenience. No online publishing/upload, always-on telemetry, webcam/microphone recording, automatic third-party cloud sharing or new replay editor is implied.

**Mandatory test report fields:** build/ROM hash, platform/driver/encoder/container, mode/course/attempt, full-attempt start/end captured presentation-frame identity/timestamp, audio/video duration and sample/frame count, A/V drift, actual duplicated/held frames vs captured/pipeline-dropped frame counts, visual/audio glitch-injection witness preserved (yes/no/timestamp), encoder and muxer result, input/ready size/hashes, maximum RSS/VRAM staging and queue bytes, average/peak disk write throughput/total bytes, median/95th-percentile save-confirmation and playable time, and guest/input/result and frame-pacing parity versus video-disabled control. Results must distinguish **tested** from **proposed budget**. No shipping claim before full exact platform acceptance.

**Scheduling:** player-valued desktop polish **after the integrated Windows core/results/records route is sound**, not a new QA-01/QA-08 P0, alternative host frontend or broad capture infrastructure. Keep the live schedule in \`WORK-QUEUE.md\`. The feature request is now explicit; this document is not an assertion that any video export already works.
