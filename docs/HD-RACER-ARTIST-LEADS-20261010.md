## Search method and negative results

Searched title aliases and game references in **English** (*Uniracers*, *Unirally*, original artist, fan model, spiritual successor, 3D render, Blender), **German** (*3D Modellierung*, *modelliert*, *Unirally Remake*), **French** (*modélisation 3D*, *Unirally*, rendered CG), **Spanish** (*modelo 3D*, *modelado 3D*, *monociclo*) and **Portuguese** (*modelo 3D*, *monociclo*, *remake*); also screened Sketchfab, ArtStation, Polycount, Unreal forums, Reddit, Newgrounds and 3D-printing portals. Exact title+Blender intersections are sparse; most multilingual hits discuss the Pixar lawsuit, ROMs, generic 3D effects or use *Uniracers* for an unrelated sim-racing brand. **Do not inflate result counts or merge identities across platforms solely from a shared username.** On a limited cross-check of existing playtest handles (TekHead, JrRobert, Swarlz-Barkley, Jonesdeclectice, cabbagery, FlyHec, Rec0deD_88, KattaTonik_), no independently credible Blender/character-rig portfolio match was established.

## Recommended outreach order and ask

1. **Owen Goss / Matt Rix:** strongest fan+3D game-production overlap. Frame the ask as a *small reference-faithful 3D model + Blender deformation rig* for a preservation/remaster, and ask whether they or their professional circle know a fan character artist. Have screenshot + brief + measurable scope, and offer paid commission, credit or an optional voluntary role without expecting free work.
2. **Pior Oberson:** share reference contact sheet and exact saddle/wheel/fork visual identity goal; request portfolio-fit assessment, paid fixed-price quote or specialist referral. Distinguish current Blender deliverable, rights and rig skills from general 3D sculpting.
3. **TamokethMB:** reach out for fan knowledge, original successor assets/prototype experience and possible community referrals, **not** as the default full-rig producer.
4. **Original team, ideally via Mike Dailly:** request advice/intro to Martin Good or legitimate visual reference, not presumed access to proprietary original model files.
5. **Bri Yu and Michaels 3D Stuff:** ask only for appropriate 3D/rig contacts or to review feasibility; skills demonstrate modeling but not exact required animation.

**Qualifying paid/volunteer work must meet the same bar:** editable native Blender file (if practical), one canonically proportioned character, independent wheel/pedal/fork controls, saddle twist/squash/stretch controls, non-destructive materials, repeatable neutral and dynamic poses, two review gates, commercial redistribution/modification rights from the artist subject to underlying game IP, and no unlicensed inputs. One unrigged-proportion milestone **before** undertaking the rig.

All identified contacts are public portfolio/community links. No personal addresses are retained. **Nobody has been contacted and no willingness to volunteer is inferred**.
