## Import policy

These are extremely useful comparison references, but this survey did not establish a license granting redistribution of the ripped Nintendo/Pixar/DMA Design game artwork. Therefore the images themselves are not copied into UR-Recomp in this pass.

They can still serve as:

- visual checks for our own ROM extraction;
- animation-frame inventories;
- evidence about sprite bounding boxes and pose coverage;
- references for identifying VRAM/OAM data;
- comparison targets for future HD asset work.

The preferred long-term approach is to extract equivalent data deterministically from the project ROM and document the extraction pipeline. That produces stronger provenance and keeps “reference art” distinct from any eventual replacement asset pipeline.
