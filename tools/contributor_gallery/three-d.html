Help shape the racer.

There's already an articulated 3D unicycle rig to inspect and improve. The wheel, frame, saddle and pedals have been reconstructed experimentally, but the character's likeness and movement still need artistic work.

What the original title-screen racer looks like

These four user-supplied references show a 3× upscaled title-screen image and interpretive outline studies. Use them to judge proportions, contour transitions, the saddle, frame, collar, wheel and pedal separation. The line work is an artist's interpretation, not recovered 3D topology.
